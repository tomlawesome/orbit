How your claims are paid

Each claim is subject to an excess of £95.00 per condition, per policy year. After the excess, we pay 80 per cent of the remaining eligible costs, up to a vet fee limit of £7,500.00 a year. Dental treatment arising from an accident or illness is limited to £300.00 a year. Cosmetic treatment, vaccinations, routine check-ups and pre-existing conditions declared at application are not covered under any Bramblewick cover level.

Upload your vet invoices at portal.bramblewickpetcover.co.uk or through our short link, brmb.uk/upload, and we will confirm receipt within two working days. Most claims are paid directly to the treating vet practice; where you have already paid the practice yourself, we pay you by bank transfer, usually within five working days of a claim being approved. You can track a claim's progress at any time by signing in to your account online or in the app.

If your vet practice is not listed on our approved network, ask them to call our vet line before treatment begins where possible, so we can confirm what is covered. Claims for conditions that began before your policy started, or during a previous break in cover, cannot be paid. Multi-pet households should quote the pet's name as well as the policy number on every claim form, since one policy number can cover more than one animal.

We record calls to our claims and customer service lines for training, quality and dispute-resolution purposes. This document, and all Bramblewick correspondence, is available in large print, braille or audio on request, free of charge. If your circumstances make it difficult to deal with us by phone or post, tell us and we will note this on your account so future contact can be adjusted.

No-claims discount

Since our no-claims discount scheme began on 1 June 2019, policyholders who make no claims in a policy year build up a discount applied at the following renewal, shown separately on your renewal documents rather than on this statement. Any claim made during a policy year, including the two shown above, resets the discount to zero at your next renewal. The discount cannot be transferred between pets or combined with other Bramblewick offers.

The Direct Debit Guarantee

This Guarantee is offered by all banks and building societies that accept instructions to pay direct debits, and has been in place since 1 January 2021. If there are any changes to the amount, date or frequency of your direct debit, Halewick Administration Services will notify you in advance, or as otherwise agreed. If an error is made in the payment of your direct debit, you are entitled to a full and immediate refund from your branch of the amount paid. You can cancel a direct debit at any time by contacting your bank or building society.

Changing your payment details

To change your bank account, collection date, or move from monthly to annual payment, sign in at portal.bramblewickpetcover.co.uk, use the app, or call 0330 111 2044. Mandate changes normally take five working days, so let us know before your next collection date wherever you can.

Complaints
