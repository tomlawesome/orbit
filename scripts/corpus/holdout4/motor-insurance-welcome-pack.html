Windscreen and glass damage

Windscreen and glass repairs are arranged through our windscreen partner, ClearView Glass Solutions, who can usually attend your home or workplace. Chip repairs carried out before they spread are not subject to an excess; a full replacement carries the windscreen excess of £95.00 shown on the previous page.

To book a repair, call ClearView Glass Solutions on 0800 021 3390 or visit clearviewglass.co.uk/larkspurbrook and quote your policy number.

Breakdown assistance

Breakdown cover is not included as standard on your policy. If you take out the £49.99 breakdown add-on, assistance is provided by our breakdown partner, Roadwise Assistance, on 0345 077 2216, or via roadwise.co.uk. If you break down without this cover, Roadwise Assistance can still attend on a pay-on-use basis.

Complaints

We aim to get things right first time, but if we do not meet your expectations please tell us. You can write to our Complaints Team at the address on the first page, call 0345 604 1177, or email complaints@larkspurbrook.co.uk. We will acknowledge your complaint within five working days and aim to send a final response within eight weeks.

If you remain unhappy with our final response, or if eight weeks have passed, you may refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service, free of charge, normally within six months of our final response. The scheme has operated since 2001.
