Important information about your loan

Your right to settle early, in full or in part

You have a right under the Consumer Credit Act 1974 to repay your agreement early, in full or in part, at any time. A partial settlement of £1,000 or more reduces your instalments or shortens your term, with interest rebated as on page 1. Ask for a figure at rgpf.uk/settle or by calling 0345 604 2210.

What happens to any insurance you hold

If you took out optional Ravensgill Payment Protection Cover alongside this loan, at £14.20 a month, underwritten by Northfield Assurance plc, settling early will usually entitle you to a refund of any premium paid for cover you no longer need, paid within 28 days. Contact the Insurance Team on 0345 850 9985.

Missing a payment

If you miss a payment we may charge a late payment fee of £12.00 and, if your direct debit is returned unpaid, a further charge of £15.00. Missed payments are reported to credit reference agencies and can affect your ability to get credit elsewhere. Contact us before a payment is due if you are struggling; there is no charge for calling.
