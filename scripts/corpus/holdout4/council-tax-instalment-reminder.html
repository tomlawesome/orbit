Ways to pay, support available, and what to do next

Discounts and exemptions

A single person discount of 25% applies if you live alone; on a Band C property like this one that would cut the annual charge by £460.67. Students, apprentices, care leavers and people who are severely mentally impaired may be disregarded when counting adults in a household. Empty, unfurnished properties may qualify for a short exemption before an empty homes premium applies; long-term empty homes and second homes can instead attract a premium of up to 100%. Apply through MyBrackenmoor or by calling 0300 123 0458; a discount is normally backdated to the date you became entitled, for example a change reported on 1 April 2024 would be backdated to that date.

Council tax support

If you are on a low income you may be entitled to council tax support, whether working, unemployed or retired. Awards are means-tested and can cover part or all of your charge; the average award under the local scheme last year was £642.18. Your existing claim reference is CTS/224417. Tell us within 21 days of any change in income, household or capital, or you may have to repay support you were not entitled to. The scheme is reviewed each 1 January; full details are published on www.brackenmoor.gov.uk. You may also be able to claim universal credit; see www.gov.uk/apply-council-tax-reduction.

If you disagree with a decision

If you disagree with a decision about a discount, an exemption or council tax support, write to the Council Tax Service at the address above within one calendar month. If we cannot resolve it, you may appeal to the Valuation Tribunal for England within two months of our written decision, free of charge, at www.valuationtribunal.gov.uk. If you remain unhappy with how your complaint was handled, you can contact the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman at www.lgo.org.uk.

Recovery and enforcement

Arrears are recovered under the Council Tax (Administration and Enforcement) Regulations 1992. Once a liability order is granted, we may recover the debt by deduction from earnings or certain benefits, insolvency proceedings, or by instructing enforcement agents. Cardwell Enforcement Services acts for us under the Taking Control of Goods Regulations 2013; fees are fixed by law at £75.00 for the compliance stage, rising sharply once an agent attends. Case reference CES-2026-05531 would be quoted in any further enforcement correspondence.

Data protection and how we use your information

We use your information to bill and collect council tax, to check entitlement to discounts, exemptions and support, and to prevent and detect fraud, sharing data with the Department for Work and Pensions, the Valuation Office Agency and other public bodies where the law allows.
