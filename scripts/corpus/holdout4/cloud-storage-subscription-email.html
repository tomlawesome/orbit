What's included in Vault Personal 2 TB

Your plan keeps every photo, document and video backed up automatically from an unlimited number of devices, with version history going back 180 days so you can restore an earlier copy of a file if it is changed or deleted by mistake. Files are protected in transit and at rest with end-to-end encryption, and you can turn on two-factor authentication from your account security settings for an extra layer of protection against anyone else signing in. Shared folders let you collaborate with up to ten people without using any of their own storage allowance, and our desktop and mobile apps keep everything in sync in the background without you needing to think about it.

If you ever need to speed things up, our support team can talk you through bulk uploading from an old computer or another provider, and our help centre at fenwoldvault.com/help has step-by-step guides for moving between devices.

Fenwold Vault also works well alongside other tools you may already use: our desktop app can watch a folder that a scanner or a photo app saves into, and our browser extension lets you drag a file straight from a web page into your Vault without downloading it first. None of this changes what you are charged; it is all included in Vault Personal 2 TB at no extra cost.
