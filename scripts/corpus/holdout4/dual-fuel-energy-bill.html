Priority Services Register

The Priority Services Register is free for customers who are of pensionable age, disabled, chronically ill, recovering from injury, deaf, blind, or otherwise vulnerable, including a mental health crisis. Benefits include advance notice of power cuts, a password scheme for doorstep callers, and nominating someone to receive your bills. Sign up on 0800 316 5029, or by textphone on 0800 316 5030. Eligibility follows Ofgem's Consumer Vulnerability guidance, updated 1 April 2025.

Using less energy

Small changes add up: wash at 30 degrees, turn appliances off standby, and draught-proof windows and doors. For free, independent advice visit the Energy Saving Trust at www.energysavingtrust.org.uk, or Citizens Advice at www.citizensadvice.org.uk, or compare tariffs at www.switchmyenergy.co.uk. Struggling to pay? The Vale Warmth Fund, our hardship trust, has helped clear over £2 million of energy debt since 2016.

Smart meters and heat pumps

Haven't gone smart yet? Book a free upgrade through KVE Smart at my.kestrelvale.co.uk/smart. Considering a heat pump? We work with installer partners under the government's Boiler Upgrade Scheme, offering grants of up to £7,500 towards an air source heat pump; see my.kestrelvale.co.uk/heatpumps.
