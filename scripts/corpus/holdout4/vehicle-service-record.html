Tyre depths are measured with a calibrated tread depth gauge at three points across each tyre; the lowest reading is recorded above. Brake measurements are taken with a micrometer on the removed pads and discs where the wheel is off, and by visual gauge otherwise. Battery testing uses a conductance tester rather than a simple voltage check. Torque figures are checked against the manufacturer's stated settings using a calibrated torque wrench, recalibrated every 12 months by an outside laboratory.

Advisory notes

Rear tyres are approaching the point where we would recommend replacement; both measured within a few millimetres of the legal minimum of 1.6mm across the central three-quarters of the tread. We would suggest rechecking these at your next visit, or sooner if you notice reduced grip in wet weather.

Rear brake pads have moderate wear remaining and should be rechecked at the next service; we would not expect them to need attention before then under normal use. Brake fluid was found to have absorbed a small amount of moisture, which is normal after several years in service, and has been renewed as part of today's work.

A small stone chip was noted at the edge of the windscreen, outside the driver's primary viewing area. This does not currently require a repair but is worth keeping an eye on, particularly before any long motorway journeys.

When the rear tyres are due for replacement, our recommended fitment for this model is the Corvane range we carry in stock; see sheet 4 for details of tyre fitting and our other services. We are happy to quote for a pair or a full set at your convenience, and can order stock in for next-day fitting in most cases.

This inspection is a visual and measured check carried out at the time of your visit. It is not a warranty against future failure of any component, and does not replace a statutory test where one is due.
