Byelaws and conditions this licence is subject to

Byelaws version dated 1 January 2025. The current version always applies and is published at fishpass.uk/rules, whatever edition is shown here.
1. This licence permits fishing for freshwater fish and non-migratory trout using not more than 2 rods at any one time, except where a three-rod licence (£59.90) has been bought instead. 2. A separate salmon and sea trout licence (£86.00) is required to fish for salmon, sea trout or migratory trout in any water in England or Wales. 3. The close season for rivers, streams, drains and canals runs from 15 March to 15 June inclusive. No coarse fishing during that period. Stillwaters are exempt unless a fishery's own rules say otherwise. 4. Undersized fish must go back without delay. As a byelaw minimum, barbel under 30cm, pike under 45cm and chub under 25cm must not be retained or removed from the waterside. 5. An unhooking mat must be used when landing pike, barbel or any fish over 4lb, and wetted hands or a wetted sling must be used when handling any fish. 6. Keepnets, where used, must be soft knotless mesh not less than 20mm, at least 2.5m long, and fish must not stay in a keepnet longer than necessary for a competition weigh-in. 7. A knotless, rubber or micromesh landing net must be used for all predatory and specimen fish; stink bags and keepsacks are not permitted. 8. A junior licence for anglers under 13 is issued free of charge (£0.00). A concessionary licence for holders of a qualifying disability benefit or the state pension costs £23.90 on production of proof of entitlement. 9. Short-duration licences suit occasional visits: an 8-day licence costs £16.00 and a 1-day licence costs £7.10. Neither can later be upgraded into an annual licence. 10. If this licence is lost, damaged or not received, a replacement can be issued for a £5.00 administration charge on production of your order number. 11. Fishing without a valid licence covering the water and number of rods in use is an offence under the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975. An officer may offer a fixed penalty of £100.00 instead of referring the matter for prosecution. 12. On conviction, a fine of up to £2,500.00 may be imposed for each rod fished without a licence, plus any costs awarded against the offender. 13. This licence confers no right to fish any particular water. A separate permit, club book or day ticket from the fishery owner, occupier or club is always required as well. 14. Nothing here overrides a fishery's own close times, bag limits or method restrictions, which may be stricter than the byelaw minimum above. 15. The Board may amend these byelaws between print runs; the version in force on the day you fish applies, not the one printed here.

Get more from your licence

Angling clubs and day tickets

Your licence lets you fish any water you already have permission for. Most stillwaters and club stretches also need a day ticket or club book. A typical club day ticket costs around £11.00, and annual club membership is often around £78.00, usually with junior and concessionary discounts. Find waters near you through affiliated clubs listed at fishpass.uk/clubs.

Find fisheries with Riverfinder

Riverfinder maps day-ticket fisheries, club stretches and access points, with catch reports from other anglers. Search "Riverfinder" on the App Store or Google Play, or visit fishpass.uk/riverfinder. Follow @riverfinderapp for river-level alerts.

Tackle partner offer

Our tackle partner Castmaster Tackle is offering licence holders 10% off rods, reels and terminal tackle in branch and online at castmaster-tackle.co.uk, using the code on your order confirmation email. Offer ends 30 April 2026 and excludes clearance lines and waders.

Young Anglers Passport

Every junior licence holder (free, £0.00) can join the Young Anglers Passport programme: free coaching days, a logbook of waters fished, and a certificate at ten stamps. Parents or guardians can sign a child up at fishpass.uk/junior.

Volunteer as a Riverkeeper

Riverkeeper Volunteers help with bank clearance, invasive species removal and fish counts most weekends. No experience is needed and training is provided. Sign up at fishpass.uk/volunteer. The scheme is run jointly with the Anglers' Welfare Trust, an angling charity founded in 1903 that supports anglers and their families after illness or injury, at anglerswelfaretrust.org.uk.

Refunds and cancellations

You can cancel within 14 days of purchase for a full refund, provided the licence has not yet started and you have not used it to fish. Refunds are returned to the card or account used to pay, usually within 5 to 10 working days. Licences can also be bought in person at a Post Office branch counter, where a small counter handling fee may apply.

Accessibility

This document is available in large print, braille or audio on request. Textphone users can call 18001 0345 601 4567. BSL video relay is available through our website. Riverbank access notes for wheelchair users are at fishpass.uk/access.

Complaints

If something has gone wrong, contact customer services on 0345 601 4567 (calls cost 7p per minute plus your provider's access charge) or licences@fishpass.uk. If we cannot resolve it together, you can ask the independent Waterways Users' Adjudicator to review your case, free of charge, at waterwaysadjudicator.org.uk.

Data protection
