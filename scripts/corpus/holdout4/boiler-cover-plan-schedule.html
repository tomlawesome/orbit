Cancellation and cooling-off

You have 14 days from the start date of your plan, or from the day you receive your plan documents if later, to cancel and receive a full refund, provided no claim has been made. After the cooling-off period, you may cancel at any time, but a cancellation fee of £30.00 applies, together with the cost of any inspection or repair already carried out. Refunds for the remainder of a paid period are calculated on a pro-rata basis less any charges due.
