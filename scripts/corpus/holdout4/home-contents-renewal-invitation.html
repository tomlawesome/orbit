Renewing, changing your mind, and other products

How to renew

If you are happy with the details on sheets 1 and 2, you do not need to do anything and your cover will renew automatically on 14 November 2026. To make a change first, sign in at my.nethergate-cover.co.uk, use our short link ngt.link/renew26, or search "Nethergate Home+" in the App Store or Google Play.

How to cancel

You can cancel at any time by writing to us or calling 0345 601 4477. If you cancel within 14 days of your renewal date, 14 November 2026, this is your cooling-off period under the Consumer Insurance (Disclosure and Representations) Act, and you will receive a full refund provided you have not made a claim. After the cooling-off period, a cancellation fee of £35.00 applies and any refund is calculated on a daily basis. Refunds are paid to sort code 40-18-22, account number 71029384, unless you tell us otherwise.
