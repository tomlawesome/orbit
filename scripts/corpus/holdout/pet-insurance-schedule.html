What Is Not Covered

This policy does not cover: pre-existing conditions present or showing signs before 1 April 2026; routine or preventive treatment, including vaccinations and worming; dental treatment unless the optional dental add-on is shown on this schedule; costs arising from breeding or pregnancy; cosmetic procedures; and claims made after the period of cover shown above has ended.

How to Claim

Submit your claim form within 30 days of treatment, together with the itemised invoice from your vet. Claims line: 0808 157 0142. Quote your claims reference, which begins TPC-CLM- followed by your policy number.

Your Right to Cancel

You have a 14-day cooling-off period, starting on the day the policy begins or the day you receive your policy documents, whichever is later. If you cancel after this period, a proportional charge for the time you have been covered will apply, and any instalments already collected are non-refundable in part.
