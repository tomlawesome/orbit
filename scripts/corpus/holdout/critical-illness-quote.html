What critical illness cover pays for

A lump sum on diagnosis

Pays £150,000 if you are diagnosed with a covered condition and survive the survival period of 14 days from diagnosis.

Covered conditions

Includes heart attack, stroke, certain cancers, and other specified illnesses as set out in the policy conditions.

Value for money

This plan could cost up to 15% less than a typical high-street provider's equivalent cover over the same term.

What is not covered

Conditions diagnosed before the policy start date, self-inflicted injury, and conditions not meeting the definitions set out in the policy document are not covered. No claim is payable for a condition not surviving the 14-day survival period.
