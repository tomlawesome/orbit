Frequently asked questions about your order

What if I'm not in on installation day?

If nobody aged 18 or over is available for your booked slot on 13 January 2027, our installer, Ryeland Field Services, won't be able to complete the visit, and a missed appointment charge of £65.00 may apply. If your plans change, rearrange the visit at my.fibrelark.uk/orders at least 24 hours beforehand at no cost.

What does the engineer do?

The engineer runs a new fibre lead from the Cotwell Fibre network cabinet or pole into your property, fits a small optical network terminal on an internal wall (serial FLK-2291-8834), and connects your Vantis router (serial VTS-40119227). Most visits take one to two hours and involve some light drilling; the engineer will discuss the best route with you before starting.

Do I need a phone line?

No — this is a full-fibre service and does not need an existing phone line. If you'd like to keep a landline number, line rental is available for an extra £6.00 a month and can be added from your account at any time.

Can I add TV or a faster speed later?

Yes. You can add the TV package for £12.00 a month, or move to our faster tier at £41.00 a month, at any point during your contract from my.fibrelark.uk/orders. Changing tier does not restart your 24 month minimum term.

What if I want to leave early?
