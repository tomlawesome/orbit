Park rules summary

The park rules cover parking, pet control, quiet hours between 23:00 and 07:00, the condition of decking and skirting, and the type of unit permitted on each row. A full copy of the rules is issued on first occupation and displayed on the park noticeboard. Rules are reviewed at least once a season and any change is notified to licence holders at least 28 days before it takes effect.

Selling or leaving your unit

A licence holder wishing to sell their static unit in situ must first offer the park the right to purchase at a fair market value; if the park declines, the holder may sell to a buyer of their choosing subject to the buyer entering a new pitch licence with the park. The park's commission on an assisted sale is 15% of the agreed price. A unit removed from the park at the end of a licence must be off the pitch within 21 days of the licence ending.

Insurance and safety

The holder must hold buildings insurance on the static unit for the full replacement value throughout the licence period and provide a current schedule to the park office on request. Gas and electrical installations within the unit are the holder's own responsibility; the park's own gas and electrical checks referred to on sheet 1 cover communal areas only. A holder letting their unit to paying guests must hold separate public liability cover and notify the office before doing so.
