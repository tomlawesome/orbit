Frequently asked questions

Can I add seats mid-term? Yes. Extra seats are added to workspace WS-2291 immediately and billed at the £8.50 per-seat list price, pro-rated to the end of the current 12 month term, which runs to 4 October 2027.

Can I move between plans? Yes. Moving from this plan to Business at £39.00 a month, or to Enterprise from £2,400 a year, takes effect immediately, with a credit for the unused part of the current term set against the new plan's first invoice.

What happens to extra storage if I downgrade? Extra storage bought at £4.00 a month continues until the end of the period it was bought for; it is not carried over automatically onto a lower plan.
