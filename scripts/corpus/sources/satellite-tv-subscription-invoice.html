Minimum term & cancellation

Packages run on a 12-month minimum term. Cancelling within 14 days of a new installation carries no early disconnection charge, though a £35.00 equipment collection fee may apply if a box or dish is not returned.

Direct Debit Guarantee

This Guarantee is offered by all banks and building societies that accept instructions to pay Direct Debits. If there is any change to the amount, date or frequency of your Direct Debit, we will notify you 10 working days in advance. If an error is made, you are entitled to a full and immediate refund from your bank or building society. If you receive a refund you are not entitled to, you must pay it back when we ask you to.

Accessibility

Bills are available in large print or braille on request. Audio description and subtitles are available on many channels; press the help button on your remote for details. A talking box menu and a signed guide to the electronic programme guide are also available on request.

Complaints

If something goes wrong, contact us first on 0800 220 1187. If we can't resolve your complaint within 8 weeks, or you are unhappy with our final response, you may refer it free of charge to Ofcom's approved alternative dispute resolution scheme for pay TV providers. Your complaint reference will begin SVTV-CC- followed by a six-digit number.

We aim to acknowledge every complaint within 5 working days and to give a full response within 8 weeks. A written summary of your complaint and our response is always available on request.

Other ways to get in touch

Webchat is available through your online account, 8am–9pm every day. Write to Customer Care, PO Box 774, Wexbridge, WX9 9ZZ, or follow @svtvhelp on social media for service updates. Please allow 10 working days for a written reply.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book an engineer? Book online at myaccount.starviewtv.example/book or call us; most visits are available within 5 working days.

What happens if my Direct Debit fails? We'll try again within 5 working days. A second failed attempt may incur a £10.00 failed-payment fee and could lead to your service being suspended until payment is received.

Can I take my box with me if I move house? Yes, but a new engineer visit is usually needed to reposition your satellite dish; standard installation charges may apply at the new address.

Can I put my account on hold? Yes, for up to 3 months a year if a property is unoccupied; a small holding fee of £5.00/month applies while your account is paused.

How do I add a second box? Add multiscreen to an existing box, or book an engineer to fit a second box in another room, from your online account.
