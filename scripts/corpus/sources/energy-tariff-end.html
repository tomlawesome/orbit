Thank you for being a Kittiwake Energy customer. We are writing to let you know that the fixed-price plan you joined on 3 October 2024, Kittiwake Fixed October 2024, is coming to the end of its term. This letter explains what happens next and the choices available to you.

Your current plan guarantees the unit rates and standing charges you pay for both your gas and your electricity for as long as it runs. Based on our records, your fixed price ends on 14 November 2026, and from the next day your supply will move automatically onto our standard variable tariff, unless you choose a new plan with us before then.

If you would rather avoid moving onto the standard variable tariff, or you are thinking of switching to a different supplier, we would ask that you let us know by 31 October 2026. Choosing a new plan by this date, whether with us or elsewhere, means the change can be arranged smoothly and without any gap in your supply.

To help you decide, the table below sets out what you are paying now on your current fixed plan, what you would pay if you took no action and moved onto our standard variable tariff, and what we are able to offer you if you fix your prices again today.

Your current plan, Kittiwake Fixed October 2024 £1,284.00 a year

A new fixed plan, Kittiwake Fixed November 2026 (12 months, ending 14 November 2027) £1,336.00 a year
