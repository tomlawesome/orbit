Waiting periods and exclusions

No benefit is payable for treatment received before your plan start date shown on the front of this certificate. A new member joining any FeelGood plan serves a waiting period of eight weeks before claiming for optical treatment and twelve weeks before claiming for physiotherapy, unless the plan is a straight transfer from another FeelGood plan with no break in cover. Cosmetic treatment, treatment received outside the United Kingdom, and treatment for a condition diagnosed before you joined are not covered under any FeelGood plan.

How a claim is paid

Send your receipt within three months of treatment, by post or through the online account. Claims are usually paid within ten working days of receipt. Payment is made by bank transfer to the member's own account; we do not pay claims by cheque. A claim above £150 in a single visit may be asked to provide the treating practitioner's registration number before payment is released.

Cancelling your plan

You may cancel within 14 days of your plan start date for a full refund of premiums paid, provided no claim has been made. After that, you may cancel at any time by giving 30 days' notice in writing or online; premiums already collected are not refunded. Bramwell Friendly Society may end a plan on 60 days' notice if premiums have not been paid for three consecutive months.

Direct debit guarantee

This guarantee is offered by all banks and building societies that accept instructions to pay direct debits. If the amount to be paid or the payment date changes, Bramwell Friendly Society will notify you at least three working days in advance. If an error is made in the payment of your direct debit, you are entitled to a full and immediate refund from your bank or building society. You can cancel a direct debit at any time by contacting your bank or building society.
