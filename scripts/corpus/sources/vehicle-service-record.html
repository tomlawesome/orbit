What a full service covers, and your warranty

What we check

A full service at Pennard Autocentre covers an oil and filter change, a check of all fluid levels, a visual inspection of brakes, tyres, suspension and steering components, a check of lights and electrical systems, and a road test. Findings that do not need immediate work are recorded as advisories, as shown on the previous page.

Vehicle identification number VRN4471029912207 and the last MOT test number, 9912 2078 4471, are recorded against this job card for our records and are not needed for routine enquiries.

Parts and labour warranty

Parts fitted by Pennard Autocentre carry a twelve month parts and labour warranty from the date of fitting, unless a different period is stated on the invoice. For the work on this invoice, that warranty runs until 20 September 2027, one day before your next service falls due.

Where we fit a part supplied through the Autocare Network buying group, that part may also carry the Autocare Network's own warranty scheme, details of which are available at autocarenetwork.co.uk/warranty. This is in addition to, not instead of, our own warranty.

Genuine and equivalent parts

Where possible we fit parts to the original specification of your vehicle's manufacturer, Verano. Where a manufacturer part is not economical or readily available, we may fit an equivalent part from Kelso Motor Factors, Tarnol or Ridgeway Tyres, each a supplier we have used for many years.

Booking your next visit

You can book your next service, MOT or repair at pennardauto.co.uk/booking, by calling 01225 960442, or using the short link pnrd.co/mot for MOT bookings specifically. A courtesy car is available for booked work at £15.00 a day, subject to availability.

Our other services

  • MOT testing, including free retest within ten working days of a fail
  • Air conditioning service and re-gas
  • Full diagnostics using manufacturer-level equipment
  • Tyre fitting and balancing, including Ridgeway Tyres stock
  • Cambelt and water pump replacement, ahead of the 2029 or 100,000 mile interval

How we price a job

Our standard labour rate is £60.00 an hour, charged in tenths of an hour, plus the cost of parts at the prices shown on your invoice. Where a job is quoted in advance, such as the £268.40 advisory work on this visit, the quoted price is held for thirty days from the date of the quote.

Loyalty discount

Customers who have used Pennard Autocentre for more than one service receive a loyalty discount on labour, shown as a deduction on the invoice; on this visit that discount was £20.00. The discount does not apply to MOT fees, which are set independently of our labour rate.

Recalls and manufacturer bulletins

We check for open manufacturer recalls affecting your vehicle, identified by engine number K9K-22908 and VIN VRN4471029912207, at every visit. No open recall was found for this vehicle at the time of this service.

Courtesy cars and collection

A courtesy car can be booked in advance for £15.00 a day, subject to a valid driving licence and availability. We can also collect and return your vehicle within a five mile radius of Trowbridge at no extra charge, by arrangement.

Understanding your advisory notes

An advisory is not a fault that needs immediate attention, but something we recommend you keep an eye on before your next visit. Tyre tread depths are measured across the tyre and the lowest reading is recorded; the legal minimum in Great Britain is 1.6mm across the centre three-quarters of the tread.
