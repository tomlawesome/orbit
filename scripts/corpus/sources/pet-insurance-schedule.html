Your Benefits In Detail

Vet fees cover diagnosis, treatment and surgery for illness and injury, including prescription food while your pet is being treated for an eligible condition, and referral to a specialist vet where your usual vet recommends it. Complementary treatment includes physiotherapy, hydrotherapy and osteopathy provided by a suitably qualified practitioner following referral by a vet.

What Is Not Covered

This policy does not cover: pre-existing conditions present or showing signs before 1 April 2026; routine or preventive treatment, including vaccinations and worming; dental treatment unless the optional dental add-on is shown on this schedule; costs arising from breeding or pregnancy; cosmetic procedures; and claims made after the period of cover shown above has ended.

How to Claim

Submit your claim form within 30 days of treatment, together with the itemised invoice from your vet. Claims line: 0808 157 0142. Quote your claims reference, which begins TPC-CLM- followed by your policy number.

Renewal & Ongoing Cover

This is a 12-month policy that renews automatically each year unless you tell us otherwise. At each renewal, your premium may change to reflect your pet's age, your claims history, and general changes in veterinary treatment costs. We will always write to you before your renewal date to confirm your new premium and any changes to your cover.

Your Right to Cancel

You have a 14-day cooling-off period, starting on the day the policy begins or the day you receive your policy documents, whichever is later. If you cancel after this period, a proportional charge for the time you have been covered will apply, and any instalments already collected are non-refundable in part.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with our service, write to the Customer Relations Team at the address below. If we cannot resolve things to your satisfaction, you may refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service. We aim to acknowledge your complaint within 5 working days and to give you a final response within 8 weeks.

Choosing the Right Level of Cover

Essential suits owners looking for accident cover and lower-cost illness treatment. Standard, the level shown on this schedule, suits most dogs and cats and covers most common conditions vets see. Premier is aimed at breeds prone to more expensive ongoing conditions, or owners who want the highest available limits.

How the Excess Works

Your excess applies once per condition, per year of insurance, rather than once per claim. If Bramble needs three visits for the same ongoing condition within a policy year, the excess is only taken from the first accepted claim for that condition; further visits for the same condition are settled without a further excess deduction.

Co-payment for Older Pets

For pets aged 8 or over at the start of the period of cover, a 20% co-payment applies to each claim, in addition to your excess. This does not apply to Bramble this year, as he is under 8 at the start of this period of cover.

Multi-pet Discount

Insure a second pet with us and receive a 10% discount on that pet's premium, for as long as both policies remain active together. The discount is applied automatically at each renewal.

Making the Most of Your Policy

Keep receipts and itemised invoices from every visit, even for conditions you don't plan to claim for, in case they become relevant to a later claim. Register your pet's microchip details with us so we can confirm ownership quickly if you ever need to claim after your pet goes missing.

Vet Fee Helpline
