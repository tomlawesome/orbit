Terms, cancellation and your data

Cancelling your plan

You can cancel at any time from Account > Manage plan. You'll keep access until the end of the billing period you've already paid for; we do not offer part-month refunds when you cancel partway through a period.

Changing plan

Upgrades take effect immediately and are charged pro-rata for the rest of the current period. Downgrades take effect from your next renewal date, so you keep your current plan's features until then. You can switch plan as often as once a day, though we'd expect most people to change far less often than that.

Keeping your account secure

Never share your password or one-time codes with anyone, including someone claiming to be from N+ support. We will never call and ask for your full password.

Your data

We use your account and viewing data to run your subscription, recommend titles and meet our legal obligations, as set out in our privacy policy at northlightplus.example/privacy. You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, from Account > Privacy. Requests are usually completed within 30 days.

Family sharing

N+ Standard and N+ Ultra can be used on up to 2 devices watching at the same time; N+ Basic is limited to 1 device at a time. Trying to watch on an extra device will pause the oldest active stream. Sharing your account outside your own household may result in a request to verify your address.

Write to us

You can also write to N+ Customer Care, PO Box 2299, Brentmoor, BM1 9ZZ. Please allow 10 working days for a reply and include your account number so we can find your account quickly.

Bereavement and financial hardship

If you're dealing with a bereavement or financial hardship, our support team can pause billing, arrange a payment plan, or close an account without the usual notice period — contact us and we'll talk you through your options.

Content ratings

Every title carries a BBFC-style age rating and a short content guidance note in the info panel before you press play, so you can decide what's right for your household and set up profile restrictions accordingly.

Data usage

Streaming uses roughly 1GB per hour in standard definition, 3GB per hour in HD and up to 7GB per hour in 4K — worth checking against any home broadband data cap before you binge a whole series.
