Understanding your new band

Every home in England is placed in one of eight valuation bands, A to H, based on an estimate of its capital value at 1 April 1991. Your Council Tax charge is a fixed proportion of the Band D charge set by each charging body, so a change of band changes every part of the bill, not just the Council's own share. The Valuation Office Agency, not Wexmoor District Council, decides which band applies; the Council simply bills and collects the amount that follows from it. You can read a general explanation of how bands work at gov.uk/council-tax-bands.

If you want to appeal the band

If you think the Valuation Office Agency's decision is wrong, you can make a proposal to have the band reconsidered by contacting the Valuation Office Agency directly, or you can find out how at valuation-office.gov.uk/appeals. Any such proposal relating to this notice should reach the Valuation Office Agency no later than 6 December 2026. If the Valuation Office Agency does not resolve matters to your satisfaction, you may then be able to take the matter to the independent Valuation Tribunal Service. Making a proposal or an appeal does not entitle you to withhold payment of the instalments shown on this bill while the matter is considered; if your appeal succeeds, any overpayment will be refunded or credited to your account.

Discounts, exemptions and reductions

Single person discount. A 25% discount applies where only one adult lives in a property as their main home. This discount is already reflected in the figures above and was last reviewed on 19/08/2026; tell us straight away if this is no longer correct.

Student and severe mental impairment. A property occupied only by full-time students, or where a resident has a severe mental impairment, may be exempt or eligible for a further discount.

Annexes. A furnished annexe occupied by a relative of the person living in the main property may qualify for a 50% discount on the annexe's own Council Tax charge.

Empty properties. A property that is unoccupied and substantially unfurnished may be exempt for a short period, after which the full charge, and in some cases a premium of up to 100%, applies.

If you do not pay

If an instalment is missed, we will send a reminder asking you to bring the account up to date. A second missed instalment in the same year may cancel your right to pay by instalments, and the whole year's revised charge shown on page 1 will then become payable at once. Council Tax accounts that remain unpaid may be referred to the magistrates' court for a liability order, and recovery, including the instruction of enforcement agents, may then pass to the Council's enforcement contractor, Carrick Enforcement, or be administered on the Council's behalf by its revenues partner, Northgate Revenues Services, under department code REV/CT/09. Costs are added at each stage; a previous reminder on this account, under summons number SUM-2026-0417, had costs of £70.00 added, which remain payable and are separate from the figures above.
