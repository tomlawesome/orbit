Funded hours

Eligible three- and four-year-olds can claim up to 30 hours a week of government-funded early education during term time; two-year-olds and working families of younger children may qualify for fewer hours under the expanding entitlement. Funding is claimed through Bexdale Council and credited against your invoice as a "funded hours credit" line; it does not cover meals, nappies or consumables, which continue to be charged as below. Parents must confirm their funding code with the nursery office at the start of each term or the credit cannot be applied.

Ofsted, safeguarding and how to raise a concern

Little Acorns Day Nursery is registered with Ofsted on the Early Years Register, Ofsted URN 154823, and was last inspected on 14 May 2024, when the setting was judged "Good". Our designated safeguarding lead is available at the nursery office during opening hours. If you have a concern about your child's welfare that has not been resolved by nursery staff or the manager, you can contact Ofsted on 0300 123 1231, or the Bexdale Safeguarding Children Partnership. Complaints should be made in writing to the nursery manager in the first instance; unresolved complaints are escalated to Bramblewood Childcare Group's head office, which acknowledges within 5 working days under complaints reference format BCG-C-nnnnn.

Accessibility, general terms and how to contact us

Accessibility

The ground floor of the nursery, including the Baby Room and the main garden, is step-free from the car park; the Toddler Room and Preschool Room are reached by a short ramp. A hearing loop is fitted at the sign-in desk. We can provide key policies in large print or in community languages spoken by our families on request to the nursery office; please allow five working days. Guide dogs and assistance dogs are welcome throughout the setting.

General terms

Places are offered subject to four weeks' written notice to end a place, given to the nursery manager; fees continue to be charged during the notice period whether or not the child attends. Fees are payable in advance, monthly, whether or not the child is absent through illness or holiday, except where a funded-hours credit applies. A missed direct debit or returned payment incurs an administration charge of £10.00 in addition to any late payment charge already shown on your invoice. The nursery closes for bank holidays and the week between Christmas and New Year; no credit is given for these closure days.

Bramblewood Childcare Group was founded in 2009 and operates eleven nurseries across Cravenshire. Little Acorns Day Nursery joined the group in 2016. A recent example complaint, reference BCG-C-20458, concerning a September 2025 invoicing query, was resolved and closed within twelve working days.

Food, allergies and nappies

All meals are cooked on site and menus are displayed weekly on the parent noticeboard and on the parent portal. Please tell your child's key worker in writing about any allergy or dietary requirement before their first session; the nursery cannot guarantee an allergen-free kitchen but takes reasonable steps to avoid cross-contamination. Reusable nappies are welcome; where the nursery supplies disposable nappies and wipes the daily charge shown on page 2 applies from the child's next full day.

If your child is unwell

Children with sickness or diarrhoea should stay away for 48 hours after the last episode, and children with a temperature, chickenpox or a contagious rash should not attend until a key worker confirms they are fit to return. Fees are still payable for sessions missed through illness, in line with the general terms above; there is no separate sickness discount.

Follow us for updates on events, closures and photos of the children's activities (with parental consent) — search "Little Acorns Bexdale" on our usual social channels, or look for the Bramblewood Childcare Group app on your phone's app store.

Data protection

Little Acorns Day Nursery and Bramblewood Childcare Group process personal data about your child and family in line with our privacy notice, available at the nursery office or on the parent portal, to run the nursery, claim funded hours from Bexdale Council, and administer billing and safeguarding records. Records are normally kept for three years after a child leaves, except where a longer retention period is required by safeguarding or funding rules. Requests for a copy of your child's records should be made in writing to the nursery manager.
