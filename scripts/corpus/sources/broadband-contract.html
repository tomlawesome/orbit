Price changes

Each year on 1 April, during and after your minimum term, we increase all our advertised prices, including your monthly price, by the Consumer Price Index rate of inflation published by the Office for National Statistics in the preceding January, plus 3.9%. We will write to you at least 30 days before any in-contract price rise takes effect. If CPI is 0% or negative, only the 3.9% element applies. This is separate from, and additional to, the out-of-contract price shown on page 1, which already reflects prior annual increases.

Complaints code of practice

If something goes wrong, contact customer services first on 0330 660 4192 or via kestrelbroadband.example/complaints. We aim to resolve most complaints within 10 working days. If your complaint is not resolved within 8 weeks, or we agree it is deadlocked sooner, we will issue a deadlock letter and you may refer the matter, free of charge, to our independent Alternative Dispute Resolution scheme, CISAS (Communications and Internet Services Adjudication Scheme). Full details are set out in our Complaints Code of Practice, available at kestrelbroadband.example/complaints-code or by post on request.

If you do nothing, your contract continues on a rolling monthly basis at the after-minimum-term price shown on page 1.

Payment. Charges are collected by Direct Debit on or around the 3rd of each month, in advance for line rental and broadband and in arrears for call charges.

Equipment. The router remains the property of Alderway Communications Ltd and must be returned within 14 days of the contract ending. A non-return charge of £40.00 applies if it is not returned in working condition.

Moving home. You can normally take your contract with you if you move within our coverage area, at no extra charge and without restarting your minimum term. A new engineer visit charge may apply at the new address if one is needed.

Fair use. This package is for normal residential use. There is no data cap, but a fair use policy applies to prevent abuse that affects other customers.

Network maintenance. We may need to interrupt your service for planned maintenance. Where possible we will give at least 48 hours' notice by email or text.
