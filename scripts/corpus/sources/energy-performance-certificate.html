About this certificate

This certificate records how energy efficient a property is as a building, rather than how it is used and run by the people living in it. It gives a rating from A (most efficient) to G (least efficient). The estimated energy costs shown are calculated using standard assumptions about occupancy and heating patterns, and actual costs will depend on how the property is used.

The recommendations above are generic measures for a property of this type and construction. Indicative costs are approximate and will vary according to the installer chosen, the specification of materials and local conditions.
