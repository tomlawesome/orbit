About this certificate

This certificate records how energy efficient a property is as a building, rather than how it is used and run by the people living in it. It gives a rating from A (most efficient) to G (least efficient). The estimated energy costs shown are calculated using standard assumptions about occupancy and heating patterns, and actual costs will depend on how the property is used.

The recommendations above are generic measures for a property of this type and construction. Indicative costs are approximate and will vary according to the installer chosen, the specification of materials and local conditions.

How the rating is calculated

The energy efficiency rating is based on the standard assessment procedure used across the United Kingdom for existing homes. It considers the fabric of the building, the heating and hot water systems, and the fixed lighting, using standard occupancy assumptions rather than how this particular household actually lives in the property. Two identical houses used differently can have the same certificate but very different real bills.

Checking a certificate is genuine

Every certificate lodged on the National Energy Performance Register carries a unique Report Reference Number, printed at the top of every page of this document. The register can be searched using this number or the property's postcode at register.nepr.example, or by calling the national helpline on 01632 960900.

Why the rating can look out of date

A certificate remains valid for the ten years shown on the first page even if the property is later altered, for example by adding an extension or replacing a boiler. Property owners are not required to obtain a new certificate after such work unless the property is being marketed for sale or rent, at which point a current certificate must be made available to a prospective buyer or tenant.

Regional variation

Energy costs used in this certificate are calculated using national average fuel prices rather than a regional or supplier-specific tariff, so your actual bill may differ from the figures shown on page 1 depending on the tariff you are on and where you live.
