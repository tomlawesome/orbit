Moving in

Customers who move in and pay their first month in full before 30 September 2026 receive their second month free of charge, credited automatically to the account; this offer cannot be combined with any other promotion and does not apply to units taken over from another customer. A local van-hire partner, Fenmouth Van Hire, offers customers a first-hour-free discount on a small van when quoting unit reference SSL-BX-3390 at the time of booking.

Default and lien

If rent is more than 7 days overdue, access to the unit is suspended until the account is brought up to date. If rent remains unpaid for 28 days SSL may exercise a lien over the goods stored, and if unpaid for 56 days the goods may be sold or disposed of to recover the debt, in line with the Torts (Interference with Goods) Act 1977; any surplus after costs is returned to the customer where they can be traced. SSL will make reasonable attempts to contact the customer by phone, post and email before exercising a lien.

Security

The site is covered by CCTV recording to all corridors, the loading bay and the gate, monitored from the site office during opening hours and remotely overnight. Each corridor is fitted with a separate keypad-controlled door in addition to the individual unit padlock, and a smoke and heat detection system is fitted throughout the building, linked to the local fire service.

Fenmouth Self Storage Centre · general terms and how to reach us

What you can and cannot store

Household and business goods, furniture and vehicles with a valid MOT and insurance may be stored, subject to the unit fitting the vehicle. Food, live animals, firearms, flammable liquids, gas cylinders and any illegal goods must never be stored on site; SSL may open and inspect a unit, with notice where practicable, if it reasonably suspects prohibited goods are present.

Ending the agreement

Either the customer or SSL may end this agreement on 14 days' written notice. On leaving, the unit must be cleared, swept and locked, and the key or access fob returned to the site office; a £25.00 charge applies if the unit is left in a condition requiring extra cleaning or if the padlock has to be cut because a key was not returned.

Accessibility

The ground-floor corridor, including corridor C where unit SSL-BX-3390 is located, is step-free from the customer car park, and a wheelchair-accessible trolley is available from the site office. Please ask staff if you need help carrying items to or from your unit.

Complaints and data protection

A complaint about your unit, an invoice or a member of staff should be made in writing to the site manager in the first instance; if unresolved after 14 days it is escalated to the regional office under a complaint reference beginning SSL-CMP. SSL holds customer records, including CCTV footage of communal areas kept for 30 days, to run the site and meet its insurance and legal obligations; a copy of the full privacy notice is displayed at the site office.

Business customers

Business customers storing stock or documents can request a VAT invoice showing SSL's VAT registration number, and can arrange multiple units under a single customer number where the units are taken out at the same time. A discount of 10% applies to a second unit taken out by an existing customer within 3 months of their first agreement, applied from the second unit's next renewal. Pallet storage and forklift access can be arranged for business customers at the Penbury site only; ask the regional office for a quotation, as pricing depends on pallet count and turnover.

Deliveries and collections

A courier or supplier can deliver goods directly to reception on a customer's behalf during opening hours, provided the customer has given advance written notice; SSL does not accept deliveries left unattended at the gate and cannot guarantee storage of goods left without notice. Goods awaiting collection by a courier are held at reception for a maximum of 3 working days before being returned to the customer's unit.
