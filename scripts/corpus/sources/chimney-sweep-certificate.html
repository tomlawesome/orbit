Guarantee

Every sweep carried out by CSS is guaranteed for the interval stated on the certificate. If soot or debris is found to have been left in the flue within that period, CSS will return and put it right at no charge. This guarantee does not cover damage caused by continued use of an appliance known to be faulty, or by a chimney fire arising after the certificate date from causes unrelated to the sweep itself.

What a CSS sweep includes and does not include

Included: dust sheets laid before work starts, full brushing of the flue from appliance to pot, removal of swept soot from the property, a smoke test, a check of the carbon monoxide alarm where one is fitted, and this certificate. Debris is bagged and taken away; none is left for the household to dispose of.

Not included: repair or replacement of a damaged flue liner, removal of a bird or squirrel nest requiring specialist extraction, structural repointing above the roofline, or servicing of the appliance itself, which remains a separate job quoted on inspection and invoiced under its own job sheet number, not this certificate.

CSS does not sweep flues serving gas appliances; that work must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Where a property has both a solid fuel appliance and a separate gas fire, only the solid fuel flue is covered by this certificate.

Burn only well-seasoned wood with a moisture content below 20%, checked with a meter if in doubt, and occasional smokeless coal as your appliance allows. A smoky fire, a strong smell from the flue, or soot appearing at the appliance seal between visits are all reasons to bring your next sweep forward rather than wait for the date on your certificate.

Have your carbon monoxide alarm tested monthly using its own test button, and replace the battery at least once a year, ideally at the same time as your annual sweep. CSS tests the alarm at every visit as part of the standard sweep but cannot be responsible for its condition between visits.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be in? Yes, someone over 18 must give access and sign for the visit. How long does a sweep take? Around 30 to 45 minutes for a single flue in normal condition. Can CSS sweep a chimney that has never been swept? Usually yes, though a first sweep on an old or blocked flue may take longer and occasionally reveals work that needs a follow-up quote. Is soot mess left behind? No, dust sheets and a sealed vacuum keep the room clean; any residual dust is the exception, not the rule. Can CSS sweep a thatched property? Yes, with extra care taken around the thatch coat and spark arrestor, quoted on inspection as it takes longer than a standard visit.
