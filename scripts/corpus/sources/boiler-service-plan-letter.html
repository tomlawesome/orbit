What the plan pays for

The plan you hold is the Boiler & Controls level. The table on this sheet shows all four levels sold at present, so that you can see what moving up or down would mean. Levels may be changed at any time; a change takes effect from the next collection date and does not restart your minimum term.

Included at your level

  • An annual service and safety check by a Gas Safe registered engineer
  • Parts and labour on the boiler, including the heat exchanger, fan and gas valve
  • Programmer, room thermostat, cylinder thermostat and motorised valves
  • Unlimited call-outs, with no excess on a repair
  • A 24-hour emergency line, answered every day of the year
  • Priority attendance if the household has no heating and no hot water

Not included

  • Radiators, pipework and the hot water cylinder itself
  • Powerflushing, descaling and sludge removal — quoted separately from £395
  • A boiler more than 20 years old, or one for which parts are no longer made
  • Damage caused by frost where the system was drained down incorrectly
  • Anything found unsafe at the first visit, until it has been put right

How to reach the plan

Calls to 01632 and 0330 numbers cost no more than a call to a standard landline and are included in inclusive minutes. Calls may be recorded for training.

Complaints

Write to the Customer Care Team, 8 Foundry Row, Hallowfield, HF1 9AB, or e-mail complaints@hearthwell-homecare.example, quoting the plan reference at the top of this sheet. We acknowledge within five working days and aim to answer within four weeks. If eight weeks pass without a final answer, or you are unhappy with the one you get, the Financial Ombudsman Service will look at the matter free of charge. Its address and time limits are set out at hearthwell-homecare.example/complaints.

Cancelling

Cancel at any time by telephone or in writing; cover ends at the end of the month already paid for. If a service visit has been carried out in the plan year and the plan is cancelled inside 12 months of joining, the difference between the instalments paid and the one-off service price of £148.00 becomes payable.

Extra help

Households where someone is over 75, has a disability, or depends on heating for a medical reason can join the priority register and are attended first in a cold snap. Documents are issued in large print, Braille or audio within ten working days at no charge. Ask on the telephone or at hearthwell-homecare.example/priority.
