Moving, stopping or no longer needing a licence

Tell us before you move so the licence can transfer to your new address or, if none is needed there, be cancelled. Use the short link cbla.uk/movein or write to the correspondence address on page four. If you stop watching or recording programmes as they are broadcast, or a property becomes unoccupied, a refund may be due for whole calendar months remaining after we receive your notice. Refunds of less than £12.00 are not issued, and requests are normally settled within 15 working days, paid to the account the last instalment was collected from.

A temporary suspension of up to three months is available for medical or work-related absences; contact us before you stop paying.

Enforcement and inspection

It is an offence under section 363 of the Communications Act 2003 to use a television receiver to watch or record programmes as they are broadcast without a valid licence. Enforcement officers may visit premises believed to be unlicensed, and detection equipment may be used from a vehicle or on foot. The maximum penalty on conviction is a fine of up to £1,000, plus costs. Officers carry identification and will show it on request. A first admission may be dealt with by caution rather than prosecution. Enforcement enquiries: 0300 555 0177.

Common questions

I only watch catch-up and streaming services, never live broadcasts. Do I need a licence? Not if you never watch or record programmes as they are broadcast on any channel or service. Contact us if this applies so we can review your account.

I have sold my television. Tell us using the online form so we can update or cancel your licence as appropriate.

Can someone else use my licence at a different address? No, a licence covers one address only; a second home or a static caravan used for more than 25% of the year needs its own licence.
