1. Definitions

In this Agreement, "the Landlord" means Mr Graham Pettifer of 22 Larch Avenue, Barchester, BR2 9LH; "the Tenant" means Ms Eleanor Vance; "the Agent" means Thornfield Lettings & Management of 8 High Street, Barchester, BR1 4DA, acting throughout as managing agent for the Landlord and as the Tenant's first point of contact for all rent payments, repairs and notices; and "the Property" means Flat 4, 12 Mulberry Court, Barchester, BR3 7QW, together with its fixtures and fittings as recorded in the inventory.

2. Term and Commencement

The Property is let for a fixed term of 12 months, commencing on 15 June 2026 and expiring on 14 June 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with clause 10 (Break Clause) or extended by a further written agreement between the parties. The Tenant shall not be entitled to occupy the Property before the commencement date without the Agent's prior written consent.

3. Rent

The Tenant shall pay to the Agent, on behalf of the Landlord, rent of £975.00 per calendar month, in advance, on the 15th day of each month, by standing order to the Agent's client account. The first payment, comprising the balance of the first month's rent after crediting the holding deposit, falls due on 2 June 2026, being the date of signing. Should any instalment remain unpaid more than 14 days after its due date, interest shall accrue at 3% above the Bank of England base rate from the due date until payment. Were the Tenant to remain for the whole of the fixed term, the total rent payable would be £11,700.00.

4. Deposit

The Tenant shall pay a deposit of £1,125.00, equivalent to just under six weeks' rent, prior to the commencement of the tenancy. The Agent shall protect the deposit within 30 days of receipt under the Home Deposits Custodial Scheme and shall provide the Tenant with the scheme's prescribed information within the same period. Subject to deductions properly made for damage, arrears or breach of this Agreement, the deposit shall be returned within 10 days of the end of the tenancy.

5. Tenant's Obligations

The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Property in good and tenantable condition, shall not keep any pet without the Landlord's prior written consent, shall not smoke within the Property, and shall not assign, sublet or part with possession of the Property or any part of it without the Agent's prior written consent. The Tenant shall permit the Landlord, the Agent or their appointed contractors to enter the Property to inspect its condition or carry out repairs, on not less than 24 hours' written notice save in an emergency.

6. Landlord's Obligations

The Landlord shall keep in repair the structure and exterior of the Property, including drains, gutters and external pipes, and the installations for the supply of water, gas, electricity and sanitation. The Landlord shall provide a valid gas safety record and electrical installation condition report before the Tenant takes occupation and at the intervals required by law thereafter.

7. Insurance

The Landlord shall maintain buildings insurance over the Property. The Tenant is responsible for insuring their own contents and personal possessions and acknowledges that the Landlord's policy does not extend to them.

8. Right of Entry

Save in an emergency, the Landlord and the Agent shall give the Tenant not less than 24 hours' written notice before entering the Property, and shall attend only at reasonable hours of the day.

9. Assignment and Subletting

The Tenant shall not assign, underlet, charge or part with possession of the whole or any part of the Property without the prior written consent of the Landlord, such consent to be given through the Agent and not to be unreasonably withheld.

10. Break Clause

Either party may terminate this Agreement by serving not less than 2 months' written notice on the other, provided that such notice may not expire earlier than 15 December 2026, being six months after the commencement date. Notice under this clause shall be served on the Agent at the address in clause 1 and shall be treated as effective on the date of delivery.

11. Termination and Holding Over

If the Tenant remains in occupation after expiry of the fixed term with the Landlord's consent, a periodic tenancy shall arise on the same terms unless a new fixed term agreement is signed. The Landlord shall not seek possession under section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 by notice expiring earlier than 2 months from the date of service.

12. Notices

Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be validly served if delivered by hand or sent by first class post to the Agent at 8 High Street, Barchester, BR1 4DA, or to the Tenant at the Property.

13. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.
