About the Smart Export Guarantee

The Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) is the government scheme, introduced on 1 January 2020 under the Electricity Capacity Regulations, that requires larger electricity suppliers to offer a payment for electricity exported to the grid by small-scale generators such as your solar array. Millbrook Energy Ltd's SEG licence obligation has applied since the scheme began; the rate and terms offered under SEG are set by each supplier, not by government, and are reviewed by Millbrook Energy Ltd from time to time as described on page 2.

Priority Services Register

If you or someone in your household would benefit from extra help — for example due to age, disability, illness, or being off-grid during a power cut — you can join our free Priority Services Register. This can include advance notice of planned interruptions to your import supply and a password scheme for callers to your home. Registering does not change anything about this export statement.
