What your plan covers

Your annual service

Once every 12 months, an engineer from Crayle Heating Contractors will carry out a full service of your boiler and its controls, checking safe operation, efficiency and any parts likely to fail before your next visit. Your annual service for this plan year is due on 14 March 2027, twelve months after your last service on 18 March 2026.

To book your service, call our helpline on 0800 118 2290, use warmvale.co.uk/app, or reply to this notice using the slip on the previous page. We recommend booking as soon as your plan renews, since appointments in March book up quickly.

Breakdown cover

If your boiler breaks down during the plan year, call our helpline and we will arrange for an engineer to attend. An excess of £60.00 is payable towards the cost of each repair. Where a repair cannot be carried out economically, a one-off repair of this kind, priced separately from the plan at £199.00, may be offered instead of a further callout.

Our 24-hour emergency line, 0330 400 7717, is available at all times; calls to it cost 7p a minute plus your provider's access charge, in addition to your normal call charges. Please use the general helpline number above for anything that is not an emergency.

What is not covered

  • Damage caused by frost, sludge or scale where the system has not been maintained
  • Any part of the heating system not listed on your plan schedule
  • Consequential loss, including damage to decoration or belongings
  • Faults present before your plan started on 1 March 2022
  • Call-outs where no fault is found with the covered appliance

A service-only visit

If you would prefer a one-off service without joining or renewing a plan, this can be booked separately at £96.00, though members do not pay this charge for their annual service.

Parts and labour

Parts and labour for a covered repair are included within your plan, subject to the excess described above. Repairs may use compatible replacement parts, sourced through our network, where an original Thorburn Heating part is not readily available.

Keeping your appliance details up to date

Please tell us if your boiler is replaced, since your plan is registered against the appliance details shown on the renewal panel, including GC number 47-311-92 and serial number 22-K-991083. An unregistered replacement boiler may not be covered until updated.

How your annual service is booked

Around six weeks before your service is due, we will write or email to suggest a date. If that date does not suit you, call our helpline on 0800 118 2290 to rearrange. If we do not hear from you within a reasonable time, an engineer from Crayle Heating Contractors may attend on the suggested date, so please let us know as soon as you can if you need a different arrangement.

What our engineers check

  • Safe operation of the boiler and its flue
  • Correct functioning of all controls and thermostats
  • Condition of seals, joints and visible pipework
  • Efficiency of the appliance compared with the manufacturer's specification
  • General condition of the system, including any radiators covered by your plan

Your Thorburn Heating boiler was installed in 2014 and is now within the age range where some replacement parts, while still available through Crayle Heating Contractors, may take longer to source than for a newer model.

If you are not in when we call

If nobody is available to let our engineer in on the day arranged, a further visit may be chargeable even during your plan year, unless you gave at least 24 hours' notice that you needed to rearrange.

Renewing by different means

Most customers renew automatically by Direct Debit, reference 22910834, so no action is needed if you are happy for your plan to continue at the price on this notice. If you would rather pay by another method, or renew for a different term, contact us before the reply-by date shown on the tear-off slip.

Other plans, complaints and your data

Other plans

Alongside your boiler and controls plan, cover is also available for plumbing and drains, home electrics, and a combined home emergency plan covering several trades in one payment. A drains add-on can be added to an existing plan from £3.50 a month.

Landlords with a rental property can take out a landlord plan from £31.00 a month, which includes an annual gas safety check as well as the annual service described in this notice.

If your boiler needs replacing rather than repairing, a replacement contribution of up to £500.00 may be available towards a new appliance fitted by Crayle Heating Contractors; ask our helpline for current terms.

Priority customers

Customers who rely on their heating for medical reasons, or who are of pensionable age, can ask to be added to our priority list, which gives faster access to the emergency line during cold weather.

How to cancel

You can cancel your plan at any time by calling our helpline. If you cancel during your cooling-off period you will receive a full refund; if you cancel later, any refund is calculated for the remaining months of the plan year, less the cost of any service or repair already provided.

Making a complaint

If you are unhappy with any part of this plan, please write to Pellow Domestic Services Limited, the company that arranges and administers this plan, or email care@warmvale.co.uk. If we cannot resolve your complaint within eight weeks, or you are unhappy with our final response, you may refer it to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

Your data

Pellow Domestic Services Limited processes your personal data to administer this plan, arrange servicing and repairs through Crayle Heating Contractors, and meet its obligations to Ardmore Insurance Company Limited. Details of how your data is used are at warmvale.co.uk/renew.
