Your right to cancel

Cooling-off period. You may cancel this policy within 14 days of the start date of the period of insurance shown on page 1, or the day you receive your policy documents if later, without giving a reason. Provided no claim has been made, we will refund any premium you have paid for the period after cancellation. If you pay monthly, any outstanding instalments will stop and we will refund the difference between what you have paid and the cost of cover provided up to the date of cancellation.

Cancelling after the cooling-off period. You may cancel at any time by writing to Hedgerow Direct at the address on page 1. We will refund any premium paid for the remaining period of insurance, less a proportionate deduction for the time you have been covered and an administration charge of £25.00. No refund is due if you have made a claim during the period of insurance.

How we may cancel. We may cancel this policy by giving you at least 14 days’ notice in writing, sent to the correspondence address shown on page 1, where there is a valid reason to do so, such as non-payment of premium, non-disclosure or fraud. We will explain our reason when we write to you.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with any aspect of this policy or how a claim has been handled, contact Hedgerow Direct in the first instance. If your complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction, you may refer it to the Financial Ombudsman Service, free of charge, normally within six months of our final response.

Financial Services Compensation Scheme. Thornfield Assurance plc is covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme. You may be entitled to compensation if we are unable to meet our obligations, depending on the type of policy and the circumstances of the claim.

Data protection. Your information is processed by Thornfield Assurance plc and Hedgerow Direct in accordance with our privacy notices, available at thornfieldassurance.example/privacy. Data protection registration ZB5528417.

Accessibility. This schedule is available in large print, braille or audio on request. If you use a textphone, you can reach us on 18001 0345 604 8810. Our office at 8 Silvermead Court, Oakenfold has step-free access and an induction loop at the reception desk.
