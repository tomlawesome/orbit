If something goes wrong

Write to the Customer Care Manager at the address on sheet 1, quoting your policy number. We acknowledge a complaint within five working days and aim to resolve it within 28 days. Waiting periods and exclusions

No call-out is covered for a fault that existed before your cover start date shown on sheet 1. A new policy serves a waiting period of 30 days before a claim on any section can be made, unless the policy is a straight renewal or transfer from another home emergency scheme with no break in cover. Faults caused by poor maintenance, structural movement, or work carried out by an unqualified tradesperson are not covered under any level of this scheme.

How a claim is handled

Once you report a fault, an engineer is normally offered an appointment within 24 hours for an emergency call and within 5 working days for a routine repair. The engineer will confirm on site whether the fault is covered before starting work. If parts are needed and cannot be sourced same day, a temporary repair is made where safe to do so and a return visit is booked. You do not pay the engineer directly for covered work.
