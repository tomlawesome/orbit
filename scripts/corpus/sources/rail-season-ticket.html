Delay Repay compensation

If a train you use is delayed, Cheddleton Rail will pay compensation under the Delay Repay scheme shown below, based on the length of the delay to your journey, regardless of the cause.

Length of delayCompensation, season ticket
15 to 29 minutes25% of the daily equivalent fare
30 to 59 minutes50% of the daily equivalent fare
60 to 119 minutes100% of the daily equivalent fare
120 minutes or more100% of the daily equivalent fare, doubled

For a season ticket, the daily equivalent fare used to calculate compensation is £9.35, worked out by dividing the annual fare by the number of working days in the year. Claims should be made within 28 days of the affected journey using your season ticket number, quoting reference DR at the start of your claim number.

Compensation is normally paid as rail travel vouchers within 10 working days, or as a bank transfer or cheque if you ask for cash compensation instead. Delay Repay does not apply to delays caused by disruption announced more than 24 hours in advance, for which a separate refund process applies.
