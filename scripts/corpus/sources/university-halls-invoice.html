Guarantor

Where a resident cannot demonstrate a UK income, a guarantor resident in the UK must complete a guarantor agreement before arrival, accepting joint responsibility for unpaid rent and damage charges up to the value of one term's rent. Guarantor forms are issued with the offer letter and returned to the accommodation office, not to the halls reception. A guarantor who wishes to be released from the agreement before the tenancy ends must arrange a replacement guarantor acceptable to the accommodation office.

Applying for a different room

Current residents wishing to apply for a different room type for 2027/28 can do so from 1 February 2027 through the portal; room-type applications received before that date are held and not processed. Ensuite singles and studios are allocated by length of tenure, then by date of application. The 2025/26 ensuite single weekly rent was £171.00, shown here only for comparison with the current rate above.

Code of practice

This tenancy is let under the ANUK/Unipol Code of Practice for Educational Establishments, membership reference EDU-0442, which sets minimum standards for management, repairs and complaints handling in student accommodation.

Cleaning and inspections

Shared kitchens and bathrooms in Kestrel Court are cleaned by the halls cleaning team twice a week during term time; residents are responsible for keeping their own room clean. A routine room inspection takes place once a term, with 48 hours' notice given in advance, to check for damage and fire-safety hazards such as blocked escape routes or unauthorised electrical appliances.

Wifi and maintenance

Wifi across all halls buildings is provided by CampusConnect under the halls contract; connect using the network name printed on the sticker inside your room door and your student number as the login. Report a repair or a wifi fault through the maintenance portal at portal.aldrethbay-halls.example/repairs, or call the 24-hour hotline on 01394 552 019; non-urgent requests are normally actioned within 5 working days.

Staying over the summer

Residents who need to stay in Kestrel Court between the end of the summer term and the start of the next academic year can book a summer-stay licence from £95.00 a week, subject to availability; ask at the accommodation office from March 2027.

Complaints

A complaint about your room or a charge on this invoice should first be raised with the accommodation office; if unresolved after 10 working days it is escalated under case reference format AB-CX-nnnn and reviewed by the university's student experience team, separately from the ANUK/Unipol code of practice route referred to on page 2. A recent example, case AB-CX-0384, concerning a disputed laundry charge from the 2025/26 academic year, was resolved within three weeks with a partial refund to the resident's account.

Fire safety and emergencies

A fire drill is held once a term in each building; residents must leave by the nearest marked exit and assemble at the point shown on the notice inside their room door. The 24-hour security office can be reached on 01394 552 020 for any emergency, including a lock-out outside reception hours.

Pay online at portal.aldrethbay-halls.example/pay using your student number, or by bank transfer to sort code 30-91-04, account number 71048852, quoting invoice BLS-INV-24-1187 as the payment reference. Payment by cheque is no longer accepted at any halls reception.

Leaving early or changing room

A resident who leaves before the end of the tenancy remains liable for rent until a replacement resident is found or the tenancy ends, whichever is sooner; a room-swap request is made through the portal and is subject to a £20.00 administration charge where approved. The 2024/25 termly charge for the room type held at that time was £2,105.00, shown here only for comparison with the current rate on page 1.

Accessibility

Merlin House has four adapted ground-floor studios with level access and an emergency evacuation alarm system linked to the university's security control room; residents who need an adapted room should contact the accommodation office before accepting an offer, as adapted rooms are allocated separately from the general room-choice system.

About BLS

BLS manages student accommodation on behalf of nine UK universities, including Aldreth Bay University, and handles billing, maintenance and room allocation under contract; it is not itself a university department.

Visitors and pets

Overnight guests are permitted for up to three nights in any fortnight with prior notice to the reception desk; guests must sign in and carry a visitor pass at all times. No pets are permitted in halls other than registered assistance animals, which should be discussed with the accommodation office before arrival.

Data and privacy

BLS processes resident data on the university's behalf to manage tenancies, billing and safeguarding, in line with the privacy notice published at portal.aldrethbay-halls.example/privacy. Requests for a copy of your data should be sent to the accommodation office quoting your student number, not to BLS directly.
