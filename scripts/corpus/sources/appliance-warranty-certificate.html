Extended Warranty — what is covered Certificate EWC-2025-118823 · Sheet 2 of 3

Your cover, set out in full

This sheet forms part of your certificate and should be kept with it. It sets out what the cover pays for, what it does not, and the charges that apply to the services sold alongside it. Where this sheet and the certificate disagree, the certificate wins. The wording on this sheet is edition BWA/EW/2025-03, in force from 1 March 2025 and reviewed each year on 1 March.

What the warranty pays for

  • Mechanical breakdown of the appliance named on sheet 1, including drum bearings, the motor, the pump and the door interlock.
  • Electrical failure of the control board, the display and the wiring loom.
  • Labour, call-out and parts for an approved repair, carried out at the address the appliance is installed at.
  • Food spoilage up to £150 in any twelve months, where the appliance is a refrigerator or freezer and the failure is covered.
  • Removal and safe disposal of an appliance that is replaced under the cover.
  • One annual service visit on request, booked at least 21 days ahead.

What it does not pay for

  • Accidental damage, impact, flood, fire or theft — these belong on a household policy.
  • Cosmetic marks, rust, discoloration of panels, and damage to paintwork.
  • Consumables: filters, hoses, seals, light bulbs, belts and detergent drawers.
  • Blockages, descaling and limescale damage where no water softener has been fitted.
  • Installation faults, including a machine that has never been plumbed in correctly.
  • Use in a business, a let property let out by the week, or a shared laundry.
  • Any fault present before 14 June 2026, or reported to the manufacturer before that date.

Claim limits

Each claim carries an excess of £60.00, payable to the engineer before the repair is signed off. The most payable for any single incident is £549.99 and the most payable over the life of the cover is £1,099.98. When the lifetime limit is reached the cover ends and no premium is returned.

Cancelling

Cancel within 14 days for a full refund. After that, a pro-rata refund is given less an administration charge of £25.00, provided no claim has been made. A cancellation requested after a claim attracts no refund. Write to the administrator or use the form at bwa-claims.example/cancel.

If something goes wrong

Reporting a fault

Telephone 0345 601 8822, Monday to Friday 9am–5.30pm and Saturday 9am–1pm. Calls are charged at your provider's standard rate and are included in most inclusive minute allowances. Faults may also be reported at any hour through bwa-claims.example, where the progress of an open repair can be followed with the claim number given to you on the call.

Approved engineers

Repairs are carried out by the manufacturer's own network or by an engineer from the approved national panel. Using an engineer of your own without agreement means the cost is not met and may end the cover. The panel's booking desk is at repairfinder.example, operated by a partner, not by the administrator.

Complaints

Write to the Complaints Manager, Bellward House, 14 Mercer Row, Norwich, NR2 1QJ, or e-mail complaints@bwa-claims.example. A complaint is acknowledged within three working days and answered within eight weeks. After eight weeks, or on receipt of a final response, the Financial Ombudsman Service will consider the matter free of charge; its time limit is six months from that response. Making a complaint costs nothing and does not affect your legal rights.

Accessibility
