Making a claim

Notify Bellward Warranty Administration Ltd of any fault as soon as it becomes apparent and, in any event, no later than 13 July 2031. Claims notified after that date, or after the expiry of the period of cover, will not be considered. An excess of £60.00 is payable towards the cost of each repair. This certificate does not cover accidental damage, cosmetic defects, consumables, commercial use, or faults arising from misuse, and repairs may be carried out using non-original parts where the original is no longer available. If the appliance cannot be economically repaired, Bellward Warranty Administration Ltd will, at its discretion, arrange a replacement or a cash settlement not exceeding the maximum claim value shown above.

If you are unhappy with how a claim has been handled, write to the Complaints Manager at the address above. If your complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction within eight weeks you may refer it to the Financial Ombudsman Service.
