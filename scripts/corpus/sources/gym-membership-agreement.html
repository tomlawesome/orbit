Terms and Conditions of Membership

1. Definitions. In this agreement, "the Club" means Cresswell Fitness Club, a trading name referred to throughout this agreement; "you" and "your" mean the member named overleaf; "membership year" means each successive 12-month period of your membership; "we", "us" and "our" mean the operator of the Club identified in clause 19.

2. Membership categories. Membership is available as Full, Off-Peak, Student and Corporate, as marked overleaf. Off-Peak membership permits access Monday to Friday 09:00–16:00 only; Student membership requires proof of full-time enrolment; all other categories permit access during all published opening hours.

3. Commencement. Your membership begins on the start date shown overleaf ("your start date") and is personal to you; it may not be transferred to, or used by, any other person.

4. Minimum term. Full, Off-Peak and Student memberships are subject to an initial minimum term of 12 months from your start date. Your minimum term will end on 2 March 2027, after which your membership will continue automatically on a rolling monthly basis until cancelled by you or by us in accordance with clause 12.

5. Fees and payment. The joining fee is payable in full on signing and is non-refundable. Your monthly membership fee is collected by Direct Debit in advance on the 1st of each month for as long as your membership continues, including after your minimum term has ended. You may instead pay the annual fee in full in advance; the annual fee is discounted against twelve monthly payments but is non-refundable once paid.

6. Price changes. We review our membership fees each January; your next scheduled review is due in January 2027. We will give you not less than 1 month's written notice of any increase, which will take effect from your next payment collection date.

7. Day passes and guests. Non-members may use the Club as the day guest of a member on payment of the day-pass rate shown on the current price list at reception, to a maximum of four guest visits per member per calendar month.

8. Freezing your membership. You may freeze your membership on medical or other reasonable grounds for a maximum of 3 months in any period of 12 months, on written application to the Club Manager. No fee is payable during an approved freeze period.

9. Health declaration. You must complete a health declaration questionnaire before your first use of the Club's facilities and update it if your health changes materially. The Club may require a further declaration at renewal or after a freeze period.

10. Induction. New members must complete a facility induction with a member of staff within 14 days of their start date before making unsupervised use of resistance or cardiovascular equipment.

11. Opening hours. The Club's core opening hours are Monday to Friday 06:00–22:00 and Saturday and Sunday 08:00–20:00, save that the Club is closed on 25 December, 26 December and 1 January each year.

12. Cancellation after your minimum term. After the end of your minimum term, you may cancel your membership by giving us not less than 1 calendar month's written notice, to take effect from your next payment collection date.

13. Suspension for non-payment. If a Direct Debit payment is returned unpaid, we may suspend your access to the Club until payment, together with the applicable administration fee, is received in full.

14. Code of conduct. Members must observe the Club's code of conduct, displayed at reception, including rules on equipment use, appropriate footwear and towel use in the fitness suite.

15. Guests and children. Guests and children under 16 are admitted only in accordance with the Club's guest and junior access policy, available at reception, and must at all times be accompanied by a member.

16. Property and lockers. The Club accepts no responsibility for loss of, or damage to, personal property, including items left in lockers, save where caused by our negligence.

17. Data protection. Personal data provided on this form is processed in accordance with our privacy notice, available at reception and on request, and is used to administer your membership and for no other purpose without your consent.

18. CCTV. Closed-circuit television operates throughout the Club's public areas for the safety of members and staff; footage is retained for 31 days unless required for an investigation.

19. The Club operator. Cresswell Fitness Club is a trading name of Cresswell Leisure Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 08823410, whose registered office is at 14 Foundry Lane, Bournholt, BH3 7QP.

20. Complaints. Complaints should be addressed in the first instance to the Club Manager. Unresolved complaints may be referred in writing to Cresswell Leisure Ltd at the registered office given in clause 19.

21. Closure. We may close the Club, or any part of it, for maintenance, refurbishment or events beyond our reasonable control, and will give reasonable notice where practicable.

22. Variation of terms. We may vary these terms on not less than 1 month's notice, displayed at reception and, where practicable, notified to you in writing or by email.

23. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
