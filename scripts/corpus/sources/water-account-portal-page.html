How to read your meter

Your meter shows cubic metres used. Take a reading from the black dials, ignoring any red dial, and compare it with your last bill to check your usage.

Report a leak

A running or dripping sound near your stopcock, or an unusually high reading, can mean a leak. Call our free leak line on 0800 774 1199, day or night.

Water-saving freebies

Order a free water-saving shower head, tap inserts and a leak-detection dye strip from our online shop, delivered free to any account holder.

Understanding your charges

Your bill is made up of three separate charges. The water supply charge pays for treating and delivering clean drinking water to your property. The wastewater charge pays our sewerage partner, Marchway Wastewater, to take away and treat what goes down your drains. Surface water drainage pays for taking rainwater away from your property into the public drainage network, and applies whether or not you're on a water meter.

How your volumetric rate is calculated

Because your property is on a meter, your water supply charge is calculated using a volumetric rate of £1.9432 per cubic metre, multiplied by the amount you actually use between meter readings. This is different from an unmetered property, which is charged based on the rateable value of the home instead. You can see your last three readings, taken on 18/04/2026, 22/07/2026 and 21/10/2026, on the meter readings tab of this account.

If your estimated annual charge looks high

Your estimated charge for the year, £571.00, is based on your recent usage and may not match what you actually pay if your household's water use changes. Common causes of a higher-than-expected bill include a running toilet, a dripping outside tap, or a garden irrigation system. Our help team can talk you through your usage history and suggest a leak check if anything looks unusual.

Metering contractor

Meter installation and maintenance in your area is carried out by our contractor, Tellick Field Metering, who may need access to read or replace a meter from time to time. They'll always carry identification and, where possible, we'll give you advance notice of a visit.

Water efficiency in the home

  • A four-minute shower typically uses less water than a bath — a shower timer is one of our free water-saving items.
  • Fixing a dripping tap can save a noticeable amount of water over a year; washers are inexpensive and easy to fit.
  • A dual-flush toilet, or a simple cistern displacement device, reduces the water used per flush.
  • Watering the garden in the early morning or evening reduces water lost to evaporation.

Changing your payment method

You can move between Direct Debit, card payment and a payment plan at any time from your billing settings. If you switch to Direct Debit, we'll confirm your new reference, based on your account number 7719 0042 18, before your first collection.

Data protection and cookies

Sarn Vale Water Services Limited uses your account and usage data to bill you correctly, plan network investment with Marchway Wastewater, and, if you've agreed, to tell you about water-efficiency schemes. This site uses cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your billing preferences; see our cookie notice in the footer for details.

Keeping your account secure

We'll never ask you to confirm your full password or bank card security code over the phone or by email. If you're contacted by someone claiming to be from us asking for these details, don't share them — call us back on 0800 774 1199 using the number on this page, not one given to you by the caller. You can review recent sign-ins and update your security details from your account settings.

Moving home

If you're moving out, tell us your move date at least two working days in advance from the Moving home tab so we can take a final meter reading and send you a closing bill rather than an estimate. If you're moving into a property we already supply, set up a new account in your name rather than continuing to pay under the previous occupant's details — otherwise a final bill in their name may be raised against the property instead.

Supplying your area

We've supplied drinking water to homes and businesses in this area since 1974, and our sewerage partner Marchway Wastewater has covered wastewater treatment here for a similar period. Ofwat reviews the prices water companies can charge every five years as part of its price review process, most recently setting the charges that took effect on 1 April 2025.
