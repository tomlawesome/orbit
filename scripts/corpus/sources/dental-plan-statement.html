The Direct Debit Guarantee

This Guarantee is offered by all banks and building societies that accept instructions to pay Direct Debits.

If there are any changes to the amount, date or frequency of your Direct Debit, Northgate Dental Plan Administration Ltd will notify you 10 working days in advance of your account being debited or as otherwise agreed. If you request Northgate Dental Plan Administration Ltd to collect a payment, confirmation of the amount and date will be given to you at the time of the request.

If an error is made in the payment of your Direct Debit, by Northgate Dental Plan Administration Ltd or your bank or building society, you are entitled to a full and immediate refund of the amount paid from your bank or building society. If you receive a refund you are not entitled to, you must pay it back when Northgate Dental Plan Administration Ltd asks you to.

You can cancel a Direct Debit at any time by simply contacting your bank or building society. Written confirmation may be required. Please also notify us.

Aldermoor Complete Care Plan — benefits

Your monthly payment covers two check-ups and one hygienist visit each plan year at Aldermoor Dental Practice, worldwide dental trauma cover up to £1,000,000, and a 10% discount off any further treatment the practice recommends outside the plan. Benefits are provided by Aldermoor Dental Practice; the plan itself is administered by Northgate Dental Plan Administration Ltd, who collects your payments.

How your plan year works

Your plan year runs for 12 months from your start date and renews automatically each year unless you tell us otherwise; this year’s renewal falls on 1 March 2027. Unused check-ups or hygienist visits do not carry over into the next plan year. If you join or leave partway through a plan year, your monthly payment and benefit entitlement are not adjusted pro rata; the plan runs to the same annual cycle for every member regardless of join date.

Worldwide dental trauma cover

If you suffer an accidental injury to your teeth anywhere in the world, your plan covers emergency dental treatment up to £1,000,000 in total, including treatment received outside the United Kingdom. Claims are made through Northgate Dental Plan Administration Ltd, not through the practice, and you should keep receipts for any treatment received abroad.

If you need to complain

Complaints about your plan payments or membership should go to Northgate Dental Plan Administration Ltd at the address above. Complaints about clinical treatment should go to Aldermoor Dental Practice in the first instance. If a complaint about administration of your plan is not resolved to your satisfaction, you may refer it free of charge to the Dental Complaints Service, an independent conciliation scheme.

Accessibility

Statements are available in large print or by e-mail on request. If you would prefer appointment reminders by text rather than post, ask the practice to update your preferences.

Priority appointments

Members over 70, members with a registered disability, or members undergoing cancer treatment can ask to join the priority booking list, which offers earlier appointment slots and a named contact at the practice for anything to do with their plan.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I miss a check-up? Your plan continues; a missed check-up is not refunded and does not extend your plan year. Can I change my monthly payment date? Yes, write to Northgate Dental Plan Administration Ltd at least ten working days before your next collection. Does the plan cover a dental emergency at the practice? Emergency appointments at Aldermoor Dental Practice are offered to plan members ahead of non-members where possible, though a same-day slot cannot always be guaranteed.

Cancelling your plan

You may cancel your plan at any time by writing to Northgate Dental Plan Administration Ltd or by cancelling your Direct Debit with your bank, though we ask that you also tell us directly. There is no minimum term and no cancellation fee. Cover ends on the date your final payment was collected.

If you move house or change practice

Your plan is arranged with Aldermoor Dental Practice; if you register with a different dentist, your Complete Care Plan does not transfer automatically. Contact Northgate Dental Plan Administration Ltd to check whether your new practice offers a plan through the same administrator, or to cancel this plan.
