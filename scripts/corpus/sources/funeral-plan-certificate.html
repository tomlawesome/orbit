Your right to change your mind

You may cancel your plan within 30 days of the date it commenced for a full refund of everything you have paid. After that period you may still cancel, but an administration charge of £285.00 may be deducted from your refund, in accordance with the terms and conditions issued 6 January 2026. To cancel, write to the Plans Administrator at the address below or use the online form at willowmere-plans.example/cancel.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with your plan, contact the Plans Administrator first, quoting your plan number. We aim to acknowledge a complaint within five working days and resolve it within eight weeks. If you remain unhappy, you may refer your complaint free of charge to the Financial Ombudsman Service at financial-ombudsman.example or on 0800 023 4567, or to the Funeral Planning Authority's successor arrangements at fca-funeralplans.example.

Accessibility and priority support

Large print and audio versions of your plan documents are available free of charge on request. If a plan holder or their family needs additional support at the time of need, our bereavement support line is available seven days a week on 0330 400 1187.

Reviewing or transferring your plan

You can review or update your nominated funeral director and any special wishes at any time by writing to the Plans Administrator. If you move house, your plan can usually be transferred to a director near your new address at no extra cost, provided the area is served by our approved network.

Questions plan holders ask us · Sheet 4 of 4

What if my chosen director stops trading?

Your plan is not tied to any single business surviving; it is the guarantee set out in your plan documents that matters. If your nominated director ceases trading, an equivalent director from our approved network will carry out the funeral on the same terms.

Can I add a family member to my plan?

Each plan covers one named plan holder. A partner or relative who wishes to be covered will need to take out their own plan, which may qualify for a joint-application discount of £100 off the joining price shown on the current price list.

What is not included?

Your plan does not include third-party costs such as the doctor's certification fee, a minister's or celebrant's fee, or the burial plot or cremation fee, all of which are payable by the estate at the rate current when the funeral takes place. It also does not include memorial masonry, obituary notices, or catering for a wake, all of which can be arranged separately through your nominated funeral director.

Is my money safe?

Money paid under your plan is held independently of Willowmere Funeral Plans and Evergreen Funeral Plans Limited by Cravenshire Funeral Planning Trust, an arrangement regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. This structure is designed so that your plan can still be honoured even if the plan provider were to cease trading.

Referring a friend or family member

Anyone you refer who takes out a plan receives £50 off their joining price, and you receive a £50 shopping voucher once their plan has been in place for three months. Quote code WFP-INTRO-26 at willowmere-plans.example/refer.
