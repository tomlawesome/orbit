Your first lesson

Your first lesson covers cockpit checks, moving off and stopping, and steering control in a quiet area before we move on to busier roads. Please bring your provisional licence, which I will check before we set off; a lesson cannot go ahead without it.

Getting ready for your test

Most learners need between 30 and 45 hours of lessons on top of any private practice before they are ready for their practical test, though this varies a great deal between learners. I will let you know when I think you are close to test standard rather than at a fixed number of hours.

Provisional licence and eyesight

Your provisional licence must be renewed before it expires; DVLA sends a reminder around two months before the expiry date printed on the card. You must also be able to read a number plate at the legal distance without correction, or wearing the glasses or contact lenses you will use when driving.

Frequently asked questions

Can someone else pay for my lessons? Yes, a parent or partner can pay by bank transfer or card; the invoice will still be addressed to you as the learner unless you ask otherwise.

What if I need to change instructor? Any unused lesson credit from a block is refundable on request, less the single-lesson rate for any lessons already taken from that block.

What if my test is cancelled by the test centre? This sometimes happens at short notice because of weather or examiner availability and is outside my control. I will offer the next available lesson slot in place of the booked test slot where I can.
