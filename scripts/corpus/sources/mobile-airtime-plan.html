Also Available on Your Account

Home broadband
Full Fibre broadband is available at your address from £24.00/month (average 150Mbps) or £32.00/month (average 500Mbps), both on an 18-month term. Add a broadband line and save £3.00/month on this mobile plan.
SIM protection plan
Add SIM Protect for £1.50/month to cover the cost of a replacement SIM if yours is lost, stolen or damaged, and to fast-track porting your number to a new device. A replacement SIM is usually despatched the same working day and arrives within 2 to 3 days by post.
Family & multi-SIM discount
Add a second SIM-only line to this account for £12.00/month, or a third for £10.00/month. Each additional line keeps its own data allowance and shares one Direct Debit collection date.
Device insurance
Cover loss, theft and accidental damage from £6.50/month, with a £75 excess per claim and next-day replacement where stock allows. Cancel any time with 30 days’ notice.
Frequently asked questions
How do I change my Direct Debit date? Sign in to My Account and choose a new collection date under Billing; changes take effect from your next bill.

What happens if a Direct Debit fails? We’ll try again within 5 working days and may apply a £7.50 failed-payment fee if it fails a second time.

Can I keep my number if I leave? Yes. Request a PAC code free of charge by texting PAC to 65075, valid for 30 days.

How do I unlock my handset? Handsets bought on a plan are unlocked automatically once the minimum term has been paid in full, or earlier on request.

Can I pay by bank transfer? Yes, using sort code 20-05-17 and account number 88301144, quoting your account number as the payment reference.

Where can I pay in person? Any PayPoint or Post Office branch accepts cash payments toward your account; ask for a receipt each time.
