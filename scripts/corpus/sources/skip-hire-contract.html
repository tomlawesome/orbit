Council permits

No permit is needed where a skip stands entirely on private land, as with this booking. Where a skip must stand on a public road, a council skip permit is required before delivery; Cravenshire Council typically issues a permit within 5 working days of a correctly submitted application, at a charge of £38.00 for 14 days, payable in addition to the skip hire price. A permit skip must display permit lights overnight; SkipFinder arranges the permit on your behalf if you tell us the skip will be on the road when you book.

What you cannot put in a skip

Plasterboard, fridges, freezers, gas cylinders, tyres, batteries, paint, asbestos and other hazardous or electrical waste must never go in a general skip; Corvedale can arrange separate collection for some of these at the surcharges below. A skip found to contain prohibited items may be charged a contamination surcharge and, in serious cases, the whole load may be rejected at the tip and returned to you for re-sorting at your cost.

Surcharges are applied after collection once the load has been checked, and are added to your card automatically using the payment details taken when you booked. If you are unsure whether an item can go in your skip, ask SkipFinder or Corvedale before it is loaded rather than after collection.

Choosing the right size

As a rough guide, a mini skip suits the contents of a garden shed or a small bathroom refit, a midi skip suits a kitchen strip-out, and a builder's skip, the size booked here, suits a typical extension or loft conversion. Larger skips need more space and are not suitable for every driveway; SkipFinder can advise on the largest size that will fit your access when you book.

Service, extra charges and how to reach us

Wait-and-load and grab hire

If you would rather not have a skip on site for the full hire period, Corvedale offers a wait-and-load service: the lorry waits while you load directly, charged at £65.00 per hour or part hour, minimum one hour, and suits a single big clearance. For loose material such as soil, rubble or green waste that does not need a skip, ask about our grab-lorry partner, Fenmouth Grab Services, who can usually attend within 2 working days.

Overloading and extending your hire

A skip must not be filled above its fill line or loaded with material that prevents the lid or cover closing safely for transport; a skip found overloaded on collection is charged an overloading charge of £45.00 and may need to be part-emptied on site before it can be collected. An extension beyond the 23 September 2026 collection date, if not agreed with Corvedale in advance, is charged at the £8.00-a-day rate shown on page 1; an agreed extension of a full further 14-day hire period is charged at the same rate as your original hire.

Paperwork

A waste transfer note is issued for every load taken from this address, reference WTN-661204-A, confirming the waste type, quantity and the site it was taken to; a copy is available from Corvedale on request and should be kept if you need to show waste was disposed of correctly. General terms, complaints and data protection

Placing your skip

The customer is responsible for choosing a safe, level position for the skip and for any damage to a driveway or surface caused by its weight; Corvedale will position the skip where reasonably instructed but may refuse to deliver if the intended position looks unsafe or is not accessible to the delivery lorry. The skip remains Corvedale's property throughout the hire and must not be moved once positioned.

Cancelling or changing your booking

A booking can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the delivery window shown on page 1; a cancellation inside 24 hours, or a failed delivery because access was not as described, is charged at £35.00. Changing the delivery date is free if requested more than 48 hours in advance, subject to availability.

Complaints

A complaint about the booking itself, including payment or the SkipFinder website, should be raised with SkipFinder on 0800 552 0198. A complaint about the skip, its delivery or collection should go to Corvedale on 01568 774 220, as set out on page 1. An unresolved complaint about a SkipFinder booking can be referred to the Financial Conduct Authority using firm reference 771204 once SkipFinder's own process has been completed.

Data protection

SkipFinder shares your name, address and order details with Corvedale so that the skip can be delivered and collected; Corvedale does not use these details for any other purpose. SkipFinder's privacy notice is available at www.skipfinder.example/privacy, and covers how booking and payment data is used and how long it is kept.

Accessibility

If you need this contract in large print or another format, or need extra help arranging delivery because of a disability, call the SkipFinder booking line and ask for the accessibility team.
