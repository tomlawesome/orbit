Frequently asked questions

Can I change my renewal date? No. The renewal date shown on sheet 1 is fixed to the original registration date for this domain and cannot be moved earlier or later, even if you upgrade or downgrade your hosting plan.

What happens if a payment fails? We retry a failed payment three times over seven days. If all three attempts fail the domain is suspended, not cancelled, and can be reinstated by paying the domain restore fee shown on sheet 2 within 30 days of suspension.

Can I move a domain away from this provider? Yes, subject to the transfer restriction shown on sheet 1. Requesting an authorisation code does not itself cancel the domain or stop auto-renewal; auto-renewal must be turned off separately from the panel before the transfer completes.

Who do I contact about a billing dispute? Billing enquiries are handled by the accounts team on sheet 3, not by the technical support desk, and a dispute should be raised within 60 days of the charge appearing on your statement.
