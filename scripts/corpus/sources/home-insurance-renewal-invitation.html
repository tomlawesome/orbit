Understanding your excess

Your excess is the amount you pay towards any claim before your insurer pays the rest. A higher excess usually means a lower premium, and a lower excess usually means a higher premium. Your standard excess of £250 applies to most claims, while a separate escape of water excess of £1,000 applies specifically to claims caused by burst pipes or similar water damage, reflecting how common and costly these claims tend to be across the country.
