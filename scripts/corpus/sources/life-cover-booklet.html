1. Welcome and how to use this booklet

1.1 This booklet sets out the terms and conditions that apply to your life and critical illness policy with Ashcombe Life Assurance plc ("we", "us", "our"), referred to in this booklet as the Insurer. It should be read together with your policy schedule, which records the specific details of your cover, including your sum assured, your premium, and the dates your cover starts and ends.

1.2 Where a word or phrase has a particular meaning under this policy, it appears in bold the first time it is used in this booklet and is explained in full in section 7, Definitions.

1.3 If anything in your policy schedule conflicts with anything in this booklet, the policy schedule takes precedence, except where this booklet expressly states that a term cannot be varied by the schedule.

1.4 Nothing in this booklet is, by itself, a contract of insurance. Your contract is made up of this booklet, your policy schedule, the statements made in your application, and any special terms we have confirmed to you in writing.

1.5 This booklet does not cover every situation. If you are unsure whether something is covered, contact us using the details in section 11 before you rely on cover being in place.

1.6 We may record and monitor telephone calls for training, quality and security purposes, and to help resolve any dispute about what was said.

2. Your cover

2.1 What this policy provides

2.1.1 This policy provides a single lump sum benefit, the sum assured, payable on the death of the life assured during the period of cover, or on the diagnosis of a specified critical illness meeting the definition in section 7, whichever happens first, subject always to the exclusions in section 4.

2.1.2 The life assured is the person named as the life assured on your policy schedule. Where the policy is written on a joint life basis, both lives are named on the schedule, and cover ends on payment of a claim for either life unless the schedule states that the policy continues on a joint life, second-death basis.

2.1.3 The amount we pay on a death claim is the sum assured shown on your policy schedule, less any amount already paid under this policy for an earlier critical illness claim under clause 2.1.4.

2.1.4 Where a critical illness claim is accepted, we pay a proportion of the sum assured, the accelerated benefit, in the amount shown as the critical illness benefit on your policy schedule. This amount is then deducted from the sum assured otherwise payable on death, so the total we will ever pay under this policy does not exceed the sum assured.

2.1.5 Cover under this policy runs for the period shown on your policy schedule. We call the first day of that period the start date and the last day the expiry date. No benefit is payable for an event happening before the start date or after the expiry date shown on your schedule.

2.1.6 This policy is not a savings, investment or pension product, and it does not participate in any with-profits fund or investment performance. The only benefit under this policy is the payment described in clause 2.1.1.

2. Your cover (continued)

2.2 When cover begins and ends

2.2.1 Cover starts on the start date shown on your policy schedule, provided your first premium has been paid and accepted by us. It ends automatically on the expiry date shown on your schedule, on payment of a valid claim for the full sum assured, on the death of the life assured, or on cancellation under section 5, whichever happens first.

2.2.2 This policy does not build up a cash value and has no surrender value at any time. If you stop paying premiums, cover simply ends; you do not receive anything back.

2.3 Waiting period for critical illness cover

2.3.1 A waiting period of 90 days applies to critical illness cover only, beginning on the start date shown on your schedule. No critical illness benefit is payable for a condition first diagnosed, or for symptoms first investigated, during the waiting period. Life cover is not subject to a waiting period.

2.3.2 For example, on a policy with a start date of 1 March 2025, a critical illness first diagnosed before 29 May 2025 would not be covered, even if the underlying condition began after the start date. This example is illustrative only and does not describe your own policy; your own dates are shown on your policy schedule.

2.4 Premium reviews

2.4.1 Your premium may be reviewed on each premium review date shown in the table in section 9. A review may increase, or in limited circumstances decrease, your premium; it does not change your sum assured.

2.5 Indexation

2.5.1 If you have chosen indexation on your policy schedule, your sum assured and premium increase automatically each year in line with the increase shown on your schedule. You may decline an indexation increase by telling us in writing within 30 days of our notice; declining an increase twice in succession removes the indexation option permanently.

2.6 Continuing cover after a claim

2.6.1 Where we pay a critical illness benefit that is less than the full sum assured, life cover continues for the balance until the expiry date, at the premium shown in the confirmation we send you following the claim.

2.6.2 If the life assured dies during the notice period following a critical illness claim decision but before payment has been made, we will pay the higher of the accepted critical illness benefit and the sum assured, less anything already paid under this policy.

2.7 Other people who can act on this policy

2.7.1 Only the policyholder, or a person with a valid power of attorney or grant of probate, may give us instructions about this policy. We may ask for evidence of the authority to act before accepting instructions from anyone else.

3. Making a claim

3.1 Telling us about a claim

3.1.1 You, or someone acting on your behalf, must tell us about a claim as soon as reasonably possible, and in any event within 30 days of the event giving rise to the claim, using the claims line in section 11.

3.1.2 For example, if the event giving rise to a claim happened on 3 March 2026, the claim should be notified to us by 2 April 2026. This example is illustrative only and does not describe your own policy.

3.1.3 If you tell us about a claim later than 30 days after the event, we may still consider it, but a delay that prevents us from investigating the claim properly may affect whether, and how much, we pay.

3.2 What we will ask for

3.2.1 We may ask for a completed claim form, a death certificate or medical evidence of the diagnosis, consent to access medical records, and evidence of the life assured's identity and date of birth.

3.2.2 We may ask the life assured, or in the case of a death claim the person claiming, to be examined by a doctor of our choosing, at our expense, before we decide the claim.

3.2.3 All costs of providing evidence in support of a claim, other than an examination we have asked for under clause 3.2.2, are met by the person making the claim.

3.3 Deciding and paying a claim

3.3.1 We aim to make a decision on a complete claim within 20 working days of receiving all the evidence we reasonably need. Where a claim is accepted, payment is made within 5 working days of our decision, by direct transfer to the bank account you have given us.

3.3.2 Interest is not payable on a claim paid within the timescales in clause 3.3.1.

3.4 If we decline a claim

3.4.1 If we decline a claim, we will write to you explaining why, and tell you how to ask us to look at the decision again, and how to refer the matter to the Financial Ombudsman Service if you remain unhappy — see section 6.

3.5 If you disagree with our decision

3.5.1 If you think we have made a mistake in assessing your claim, ask us to review it, providing any further information you think is relevant. A review is carried out by someone who was not involved in the original decision.

3.6 Fraudulent claims

3.6.1 If a claim, or any information given to support it, is fraudulent or intentionally exaggerated, we may refuse the whole claim, recover any amount already paid, and cancel this policy without refunding your premiums.

4. Exclusions and limitations

4.1 General exclusions

4.1.1 We do not pay a claim arising directly or indirectly from the life assured taking part in war, invasion, act of a foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection or military coup, whether declared or not.

4.1.2 We do not pay a claim where the death or diagnosis results from the life assured's own criminal act.

4.1.3 Death by suicide is excluded if it occurs within 12 months of the start date. After 12 months from the start date, death by suicide is covered in the same way as any other cause of death.

4.1.4 We may reduce or refuse a claim, or treat the policy as if it never existed, where the life assured failed to disclose a material fact when applying for cover, and that fact would have affected our decision to offer cover or the terms on which we offered it.

4.1.5 Cover applies to events occurring anywhere in the world, but a claim arising from travel to, or residence in, a country against which the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office has issued advice against all travel at the relevant time may be investigated further before we pay.

4.1.6 We do not pay a claim arising from a diagnosis made, or treatment given, outside a recognised hospital or clinic, unless we agree in advance that the circumstances made this impossible or inappropriate.

4.2 Critical illness exclusions

4.2.1 We do not pay a critical illness benefit for a condition, or the underlying cause of a condition, that a reasonable person would have expected to seek medical advice about before the start date, whether or not a diagnosis had actually been made.

4. Exclusions and limitations (continued)

4.2.2 We do not pay a critical illness benefit for a diagnosis made, or symptoms first investigated, during the 90-day waiting period described in clause 2.3.

4.2.3 Each specified critical illness covered by this policy must meet the full definition used in the Association of British Insurers' model wording current at the start date, as summarised in section 7. A diagnosis that does not meet that definition, even if described using the same name by a treating clinician, is not covered.

4.2.4 We do not pay a critical illness benefit for total permanent disability unless you have selected total permanent disability cover on your policy schedule and paid the additional premium shown for it.

4.3 Territorial and activity limits

4.3.1 We do not pay a claim arising from the life assured's participation in motor racing, motorcycle racing, aviation other than as a fare-paying passenger on a licensed airline, or professional sport, unless we have agreed in writing to cover the activity and any additional premium has been paid.

4.3.2 We do not pay a claim arising from the life assured's use of a controlled drug other than as prescribed by a registered medical practitioner, or from alcohol misuse where alcohol misuse is recorded as a contributing cause on the death certificate or medical report.

4.4 How exclusions interact with your schedule

4.4.1 Your policy schedule may list further exclusions specific to you, agreed at the time you applied for cover. Those exclusions apply in addition to the exclusions in this section and take priority over anything in this section that conflicts with them.

4.5 How exclusions are applied

4.5.1 An exclusion in this section applies only to the specific event it describes. It does not otherwise reduce the cover provided by this policy, and a claim that does not fall within an exclusion is assessed in the normal way described in section 3.

5. Cancelling your policy

5.1 Your right to cancel

5.1.1 You have a cooling-off period of 30 days from the later of the day you receive your policy schedule and this booklet, and the day you receive our first written confirmation that cover has started, during which you may cancel this policy and receive a full refund of any premium paid, provided no claim has been made.

5.1.2 For example, if you received your policy schedule and this booklet on 3 April 2026, your cooling-off period would end on 3 May 2026. This example is illustrative only and does not describe your own policy.

5.1.3 To cancel during the cooling-off period, write to us at the address in section 11, or call the number shown there. We will confirm cancellation in writing and refund any premium within 10 working days.

5.2 Cancelling after the cooling-off period

5.2.1 After the cooling-off period ends, you may cancel this policy at any time by telling us in writing. Cover ends on the date we receive your instruction, or a later date you specify. No refund of premiums already paid is given for cover already provided.

5.2.2 If you cancel, or if a claim ends this policy, you may be able to apply for a new policy, but this will be treated as a new application, assessed on the medical and other information current at that time, and any waiting period described in clause 2.3 will begin again from the new start date.

5.3 Our right to cancel

5.3.1 We may cancel this policy by giving you 30 days' written notice if we reasonably suspect fraud, or if a premium remains unpaid for more than 30 days after its due date despite the reminder described in clause 9.3.

5.3.2 We will not cancel this policy simply because you have made a valid claim, or because your health has changed since the start date.

5.4 If the life assured dies during the cooling-off period

5.4.1 If the life assured dies during the cooling-off period described in clause 5.1, we will pay the sum assured as if the policy had not been cancelled, provided the premium had been paid and the death is not otherwise excluded under section 4.

6. Complaints and how we handle them

6.1 How to complain

6.1.1 If you are unhappy with any decision we have made, or with the service you have received, please tell us. Contact details for our complaints team are in section 11.

6.1.2 Please give us your policy number, your name, and as much detail as you can about the reason for your complaint, so that we can look into it properly.

6.2 What happens next

6.2.1 We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 working days. We aim to send you a final response within 4 weeks, and in any event within 8 weeks, of receiving your complaint.

6.2.2 For example, a complaint received on 1 June 2026 would normally receive a final response by 27 July 2026 at the latest. This example is illustrative only.

6.2.3 If we cannot resolve your complaint within 8 weeks, we will write to you explaining why, and tell you about your right to refer the matter to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

6.3 The Financial Ombudsman Service

6.3.1 If you remain unhappy with our final response, or if 8 weeks have passed without one, you may refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service free of charge, normally within 6 months of our final response.

6.4 The Financial Services Compensation Scheme

6.4.1 We are covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme. If we are unable to meet our obligations under this policy, you may be entitled to compensation, depending on the type of policy and the circumstances of the claim. Further details are in section 11.

6.5 Vulnerable customers

6.5.1 If you tell us that you, or the life assured, need extra support because of a disability, bereavement, or another circumstance, we will make reasonable adjustments to how we communicate with you and handle your complaint or claim.

7. Definitions

The following words and phrases have the meaning given below wherever they appear in bold in this booklet and in your policy schedule.

Insurer — Ashcombe Life Assurance plc, the company providing this policy.
Policyholder — the person named as the policyholder on the policy schedule, who owns this policy and is responsible for paying the premium.
Life Assured — the person on whose life or health a claim under this policy depends, as named on the policy schedule.
Sum Assured — the maximum amount payable under this policy, as shown on the policy schedule.
Critical Illness — a condition meeting one of the specified definitions summarised in this section, based on the Association of British Insurers' model wording.
Waiting Period — the 90-day period described in clause 2.3, during which no critical illness benefit is payable.
Accelerated Benefit — a critical illness benefit paid as an advance against the sum assured, reducing the amount payable on a later death claim.
Total Permanent Disability — permanent inability to carry out at least three of the six activities of daily living listed on your policy schedule, as certified by a consultant we approve, where total permanent disability cover has been selected.
Cooling-Off Period — the 30-day period described in clause 5.1, during which you may cancel this policy and receive a full refund.
Material Fact — information that would have affected our decision to offer this policy, or the terms on which we offered it, had we known it when you applied.
Premium Review Date — a date shown in the table in section 9 on which your premium may be reviewed.
Terminal Illness — an advanced or rapidly progressing illness where, in the opinion of the attending consultant, life expectancy is 12 months or less; a valid terminal illness claim is paid in advance of a death claim.
Start Date — the date shown as the policy start date on your policy schedule, from which cover begins.
Expiry Date — the date shown as the policy end date on your policy schedule, after which no benefit is payable.

9.3 What happens if you miss a payment

9.3.1 If a premium is not paid on its due date, we will write to remind you. If it remains unpaid 30 days after the due date, cover ends automatically, subject to our right to cancel described in clause 5.3.

9.4 Illustrative cost of your policy

9.4.1 If you pay monthly for the whole of your policy term without any premium review changing the amount, the total you would pay over the term is approximately £7,800.00. This figure is illustrative only: your actual premium may change following a review under clause 9.2, and the actual total you pay will depend on how long the policy runs.

9.4.2 If you choose to pay annually instead of monthly, the annual premium is shown on your policy schedule; paying annually is usually cheaper overall than paying the same monthly premium twelve times over.

9.5 Amounts shown on your schedule

9.5.1 Your policy schedule shows your sum assured, your critical illness benefit, and your premium. These are the amounts that apply to you; nothing in this section overrides them.

9.6 How you pay

9.6.1 Premiums are collected by direct debit from the bank account shown on your application, on or near the day of the month shown on your policy schedule. We do not accept payment by cash or by post.

9.7 Optional add-on and rider premiums

9.7.1 If you add total permanent disability cover, waiver of premium, or indexation after your policy starts, we will confirm the additional premium for each in writing and add it to your monthly premium from your next premium review date shown in the table above. For a policy with your sum assured, illustrative additional premiums are: total permanent disability cover, £4.10 per month; waiver of premium, £1.85 per month; indexation, approximately 3% of premium each year. These are illustrative only; the actual premium for any add-on would be confirmed before it takes effect.
