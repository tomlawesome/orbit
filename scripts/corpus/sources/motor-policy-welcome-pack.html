Making a claim

If you are involved in an accident, please stay calm, check for injuries and move to a safe place if you can. Exchange details with any other driver involved, take photographs if it is safe to do so, and note the time, location and weather conditions. You do not need to admit fault to anyone at the scene.

To start a claim, call our claims line on 0800 011 3344, available 24 hours a day (calls from UK landlines are free; mobile network charges may apply and calls may be recorded for training purposes). Claims are handled on our behalf by Lansdale Claims Management, and windscreen repairs are arranged directly with Clearview Auto Glass where the damage qualifies. You can also start a claim online at claims.harroway.co.uk.

Every motor insurer is required to add your vehicle and policy details to the Motor Insurance Database, which is used by the police, the DVLA and local authorities to check that a vehicle on the road is insured. The database is maintained by the Motor Insurers' Bureau, which also compensates victims of accidents caused by uninsured or untraced drivers.

No claims discount

Your no claims discount reduces your premium the longer you go without making a fault claim. If you need to make a claim, your discount may be reduced at your next renewal even if the claim is not your fault, unless you hold protected no claims discount. Ask us about adding protection when your renewal is due.

If you need to complain

We aim to resolve any concern quickly. Please contact us in the first instance at help@harroway.co.uk or by writing to Customer Relations, Harroway Direct, Cheltenham. If we cannot resolve your complaint to your satisfaction within eight weeks, or you are unhappy with our final response, you may be able to refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service free of charge, provided you contact them within six months of our final response.

Our complaints team reviews every case individually and will acknowledge your complaint within five working days. Most complaints are resolved well within the eight week timescale, and we will keep you updated in writing if a full response is going to take longer than expected.

Your right to cancel

You have 14 days from the day your policy starts, or the day you receive your documents if later, to cancel your policy and receive a full refund, provided you have not made a claim. After the cooling off period, you can still cancel at any time, but the cancellation fee of £45.00 will apply and we will only refund the unused portion of your premium.

Data protection. We use your information to provide your insurance, assess risk and prevent fraud, and may share it with the underwriter, the claims handler and industry databases such as the Motor Insurance Database. Driving abroad. Your policy provides the minimum cover required by law in most European countries for a limited period, usually up to ninety days. Contact us before travelling if you would like full cover extended for your trip, and check whether a green card or additional documents are required for your destination. Cover for driving outside Europe is not included and must be arranged separately.
