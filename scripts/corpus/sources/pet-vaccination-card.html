About this record — course and boosters

Primary course. Puppies normally receive two injections, three to four weeks apart, from around eight weeks of age, followed by a first booster around twelve months later. Bramble’s primary course was completed with the doses of 14 July and 4 August 2022 shown above.

Annual boosters. After the primary course, distemper/hepatitis/parvovirus/ parainfluenza and leptospirosis cover is maintained with an annual booster. Kennel cough cover is also given annually and is recommended before boarding, training classes or shows.

Vial batch and expiry. Each row above records the batch number and shelf-life expiry date printed on the vial actually used, as required for veterinary medicine traceability.

Missed boosters. If a booster is given significantly later than the due date, the vet may recommend restarting the primary course rather than treating it as a simple top-up. Please book before the due date shown on the card overleaf wherever possible.

Boarding and travel. Kennels, catteries and some travel schemes require proof of an in-date kennel cough vaccination, normally given or boosted within the twelve months before boarding. Bring this card with you when booking.

Keeping this card. This card is Bramble’s record of vaccinations given by this practice. If Bramble is treated elsewhere, please ask the other practice to record details here or to send us a copy for our records.
