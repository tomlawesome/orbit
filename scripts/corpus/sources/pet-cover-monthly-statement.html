How your cover works

Bramblepaw Pet Healthcare is the trading name you know us by, and the name to use if you ever need to call us. Your policy is underwritten by Sturbridge Underwriting Agency Limited, and claims are handled on our behalf by Vetclaim Services, who work directly with your registered practice wherever possible so you do not have to pay the full bill upfront and wait to be reimbursed.

Not everything is covered. Your policy does not cover conditions that existed, or showed symptoms, before your policy started on 01/02/2023, routine or preventative treatment such as vaccinations and flea treatment, or the cost of a health certificate for travel. Dental cover applies only to accidental damage and specific conditions listed in your policy booklet, not routine scale and polish treatment.

Our 24 hour vet advice line is available to all policyholders and can help you decide whether a trip to the vet is needed. You can also use our practice finder tool to locate a vet near you if you are away from home, and our app lets you submit claim photographs, track a claim's progress at vetclaim.co.uk/track, and view your statements without waiting for the post.

Microchipping and travel

Keeping your pet's microchip details up to date with the national database helps reunite you quickly if they are ever lost. If you plan to travel abroad with your pet, check well in advance whether your policy includes any travel cover and what documentation you will need, as requirements vary between countries and can change with little notice.

Dental cover in detail

Dental treatment following an accident, such as a broken tooth from a fall or a road accident, is covered in the same way as any other accidental injury, subject to your excess and co-payment. Routine dental care, including scale and polish treatment, tartar removal and check-ups recommended purely for preventative reasons, is not covered under any of our policies, in line with most pet insurance in the market.

Multi-pet households

If you insure more than one pet from the same household with Bramblepaw Pet Healthcare, a multi-pet discount is applied automatically to each policy, and each pet still has its own policy number, excess and cover limits. Adding a second pet does not affect Milo's cover or his policy number BP 4411 8820 6 in any way.
