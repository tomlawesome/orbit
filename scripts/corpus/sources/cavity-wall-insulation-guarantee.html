How your guarantee is backed

This guarantee is supported by an insurance-backed guarantee scheme administered by GuardBuild Warranty Ltd, scheme reference GBW-771049. If the installer has ceased trading, GuardBuild Warranty Ltd will handle a valid claim under the terms of the separate policy document issued for that scheme on 19 May 2026. A copy of the policy wording can be requested from claims@guardbuildwarranty.example or downloaded from guardbuildwarranty.example/policies.

Step by step: making a claim

  • Contact us on the number below, quoting the guarantee number shown on page 1
  • We will arrange an inspection, normally within 20 working days
  • If a defect covered by the guarantee is confirmed, we will agree a programme of remedial work with you in writing
  • If we cannot carry out the work, GuardBuild Warranty Ltd will step in under the insurance-backed scheme

Complaints

If you are unhappy with the service you received, write to the Customer Care Manager at the address on page 1 or e-mail complaints@brightwellinsulation.example. We aim to acknowledge a complaint within five working days and resolve it within 28 days. If you remain dissatisfied, you may refer the matter free of charge to the industry conciliation scheme operated through CIGA at ciga.example/complaints.

Standards and inspection

Installations are carried out to CIGA technical requirements and BBA certification, and are subject to independent inspection. The installer has held CIGA membership since 2011, membership number CIGA-M-6621, last audited 4 February 2026 with the next audit due in February 2027.

What is not covered

This guarantee does not cover damage caused by structural movement, existing rot or damp not related to the insulation, alterations made by another contractor after installation, or damage caused by drilling, fixing or rendering work carried out without the installer's prior written agreement. A separate warranty, if any, covering render or external cladding is issued by the render manufacturer and is not part of this guarantee.

If you move house

This guarantee can be transferred to a new owner of the property, provided the new owner writes to the installer within 3 months of the change of ownership, quoting the guarantee number on page 1. There is no charge for a straightforward transfer of an unclaimed guarantee.
