Terms and conditions — extended appliance guarantee

1. Who provides this cover

1.1 This extended guarantee is sold and administered by Fernhill Appliance Care, Fernhill House, Dunmow Way, Ipswich, IP2 9RR (company registration number 07719042, VAT number GB 771 2094 33). Fernhill Appliance Care is not the manufacturer of your appliance, Vantage Kitchens, and is not the retailer, Halloway Electricals, from whom the appliance was bought.

1.2 The financial risk under this guarantee is carried by Redhurst Insurance plc, which is authorised to write this class of business. Fernhill Appliance Care administers claims and bookings on Redhurst Insurance plc's behalf and is the firm you should contact about your cover.

1.3 Repairs are carried out through our approved network, Swift Domestic Repairs, whose engineers hold the following scheme registrations: Gas Safe 447102 and NICEIC 22908. Genuine and compatible parts are supplied by Kelso Spares where the manufacturer's own part is unavailable.

2. When cover starts and ends

2.1 This extended guarantee runs from 9 May 2028, the day the appliance's manufacturer's guarantee ends (8 May 2028), until 8 May 2031. It does not run alongside the manufacturer's guarantee and no claim can be made under this card before 9 May 2028.

2.2 Cover simply ends on 8 May 2031. Nothing further is due from you and there is no automatic renewal; if you want continuing cover after that date you would need to buy a new plan.

2.3 The appliance was purchased on 9 May 2026 and registered on 23 May 2026, within the thirty day registration deadline. A registration made after thirty days from purchase may be refused or may attract a processing fee.

2.4 You have a cooling-off period of fourteen days from the card print date of 26 May 2026, ending 6 June 2026, during which you may cancel this guarantee for a full refund of the £149.00 paid, provided no claim has been made or booked.

3. What is covered

3.1 Cover applies to mechanical or electrical breakdown of the appliance described on the front of this card, arising from normal domestic use, during the period stated at clause 2.1.

3.2 An excess of £35.00 is payable by you towards the cost of each repair visit made under this guarantee. A visit found not to be covered, for example where no fault is found or the fault is excluded under clause 4, will be charged at £75.00.

3.3 Where the appliance cannot be economically repaired, "beyond economical repair" meaning the cost of repair would exceed the appliance's value, we will arrange a replacement of similar specification or a cash settlement. In either case the amount will not exceed the replacement limit of £700.00 shown on the front of this card.

3.4 Settlement offers take into account the age and condition of the appliance and are not required to match the original £629.00 purchase price.

4. What is not covered

4.1 This guarantee does not cover cosmetic damage, accidental damage, consumable parts such as filters and seals, commercial or non-domestic use, or faults caused by incorrect installation, misuse or failure to descale or clean the appliance as instructed.

4.2 Cover does not extend to a second or replacement appliance obtained other than through a settlement under clause 3.3, and does not cover any appliance other than the one described on the front of this card by its serial number, 22K9910834.

4.3 Call-out visits requested outside the terms of this guarantee, including where the appliance is found to be working correctly, will be charged at the rate shown at clause 3.2.

5. How to book a repair

5.1 To arrange a repair, contact Fernhill Appliance Care on 0330 100 2291 or through my.fernhillcare.co.uk, quoting your guarantee number FAC-EG-2291083. Swift Domestic Repairs will contact you to arrange an appointment.

5.2 A repair booking is given its own reference, for example BK-44710, which you should quote in any further correspondence about that particular visit. A cancelled appointment without at least 24 hours' notice may be charged at £45.00.

5.3 Please have your registration identifier, 5510983, and your guarantee number to hand when you call, so that we can find your record quickly.

11.3 Does the excess apply to every visit, even a second visit for the same fault? No, if an engineer from Swift Domestic Repairs needs to return to complete a repair already booked, no further excess is charged for that same job.

11.4 What happens if Fernhill Appliance Care stops trading? Your guarantee is underwritten by Redhurst Insurance plc, so a claim can still be made directly to the insurer using the details on this card.
