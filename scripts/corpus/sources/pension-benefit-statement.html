Taking your pension early or late

The earliest age you can normally draw a workplace pension is 55, which for you falls on 12 March 2040. Drawing your pension earlier than your selected retirement date will usually reduce the amounts shown above, because your fund has less time to grow and your pension is expected to be paid for longer. You do not have to draw your pension on 12 March 2053; you can choose to keep contributing beyond that date, or draw it sooner from age 55.

Where your money is invested

Unless you have chosen otherwise, your contributions are invested in the Ashcombe Balanced Growth Fund, the scheme's default arrangement, managed on the trustees' behalf by Ashcombe Asset Management Ltd. You can switch funds at any time free of charge by writing to us or using the online portal at the address on page 4.

Assumptions used in this statement

  1. 1 The income if you took your pension today assumes you used your whole fund to buy a level, single-life annuity from an insurer at current market annuity rates, with no guarantee period and no dependant's pension. Actual annuity rates change daily and vary between providers.
  2. 2 The annuity used for the projections on page 3 is a level, single-life annuity with no guarantee period, purchased on the date you reach your selected retirement date. Buying a different type of annuity, or taking your money in another way such as drawdown or as a lump sum, would produce different figures.
  3. 3 Today's money figures assume price inflation of 2.5% a year between 5 April 2026 and your selected retirement date, in line with guidance published by the Financial Reporting Council.
  4. 4 The three growth rates are set out in Actuarial Standard Technical Memorandum 1 (AS TM1), version 4.2, which came into effect on 1 October 2018 and prescribes the rates that occupational schemes must use for statutory illustrations. They are not a forecast, minimum or guarantee of what your investments will actually return.
  5. 5 Annual income figures are shown before income tax and assume the annuity is paid monthly in advance and does not increase once in payment.
  6. 6 This statement has been prepared on the Statutory Money Purchase Illustration (SMPI) basis required by the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2013, a requirement introduced for money purchase pensions on 6 April 2003.

Charges

An annual management charge of 0.42% of the value of your fund is deducted by Ashcombe Asset Management Ltd, calculated daily and already reflected in the fund value and the projections shown in this statement. There is no charge for joining, leaving or transferring out of the scheme.

Risk warnings

The value of your pension can go down as well as up, and you may get back less than has been paid in. Past performance is not a guide to future performance.

The illustrations in this statement are not guaranteed. They are based on assumptions about investment growth, inflation and annuity rates that may not happen. Your actual pension could be higher or lower.

Tax relief and the tax treatment of pensions depend on your individual circumstances and may change in the future. Legislation governing workplace pensions may also change before your selected retirement date.

If you leave Corvedale Logistics Ltd, your benefits in this scheme remain invested and continue to be subject to charges and investment risk, whether or not you keep contributing.
