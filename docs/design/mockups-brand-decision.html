The mark — decision card

Not a new concept: the proposal is that Orbit's mark is the one it already uses — the gravity-well dial, miniaturised. The year is the ring, the planet is the thing approaching, the household burns at the centre. The word sets plain because the mark carries everything. Below: how much dress the same drawing can wear, from hero to favicon.

Why this is the mark: it's the product miniaturised, not a logo invented beside it. The full-dress version carries the whole identity — the year ring, the threshold, the household sun, a planet mid-approach with its trail (and on the real splash it slowly orbits) — while every reduction sheds detail in the same order the product itself does. The word never performs; it just names the drawing.

What it replaces: the three-arc gesture mark (pre-star-chart era) retires; all bespoke wordmark treatments are dropped.
