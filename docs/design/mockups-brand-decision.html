The rule: the mark is exactly three flat geometric shapes — the ring, the planet, and the plain filled centre. No gradients, no glows, no 3D, no decoration of any kind, at any size, in any medium. Colour is the only variable, and it comes from the theme tokens. Motion is permitted in exactly one place: on live surfaces the planet may drift slowly around the ring. Because the shapes are pure flat geometry, the identity survives print, embroidery, favicons and dark/light alike.

What it replaces: the three-arc gesture mark retires; all bespoke wordmark treatments are dropped; the word always sets plain.
