The rule: the mark is exactly two geometric shapes — the ring and the planet. Nothing ever sits inside the ring. The geometry is identical at every size and in every medium; the only thing that changes is dress: at hero sizes the planet wears its 3D body and glow (and slowly orbits on live surfaces), in chrome it keeps the 3D body, and in one-ink contexts both shapes go flat. Because the shapes are pure geometry, the identity survives print, embroidery, favicons and dark/light alike.

What it replaces: the three-arc gesture mark retires; all bespoke wordmark treatments are dropped; the word always sets plain.
