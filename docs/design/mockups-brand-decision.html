The rule: the mark is exactly three geometric shapes — the ring, the planet, and the plain filled centre. The centre is always a simple flat circle: no inner ring around it, no gradient, no glow — the household doesn't perform, it just is. The only dress the mark ever wears is on the planet: 3D body and glow at hero sizes (slowly orbiting on live surfaces), 3D body in chrome, flat in one-ink contexts. Because the shapes are pure geometry, the identity survives print, embroidery, favicons and dark/light alike.

What it replaces: the three-arc gesture mark retires; all bespoke wordmark treatments are dropped; the word always sets plain.
