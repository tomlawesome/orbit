Concept B′ — the word in orbit

B′ · the word in orbit — dark

Wordmark, orbit-first

One continuous ellipse sweeps the full word, low and tilted; it passes behind the letters on its far arc and back in front on its near arc. The planet riding that path sits exactly where the tittle of the i belongs — it doesn't sit near the i, it is the i's dot.

light mode — #f4f1e8 paper, ink stroke, deeper accent for contrast

single-colour — one ink, depth from opacity only, no accent needed

~200px wide
~120px wide

Geometry, not eyeballing: the page measures the dotless ı's actual stem box at render time and places the planet a fixed gap above it — the same spot a real tittle would sit. The ellipse's rx, tilt and vertical centre are chosen for proportion (wide, low, −8°); its ry is then solved so the curve passes through that exact point, not nudged there by hand. The letter is set as the Unicode dotless ı (U+0131) — a real "i" would leave its own tittle sitting right next to the planet, reading as a rendering mistake rather than a deliberate mark.

Concept C′ — arcs with a heart

strongest

Gold sun

Accent-fill core with a soft layered glow — the arcs read immediately as orbits around a warm body. Matches concept A's perihelion glow.

quiet

Ink-white core

No glow, no colour — a plain body at the centre. Confirms the arcs still read as orbits with nothing more than a dot to orbit around.

alternate

Ringed planet

A larger muted body with a thin ring — more incident than the sun, closer in spirit to concept A's household-at-centre idea, but busier at small sizes.

32 / 16 px favicon reductions — C′ with the gold sun centre

At 16px the glow is dropped and the sun becomes a flat dot — same simplification concept A's ring-plus-dot already relies on, so the two marks degrade the same way at favicon scale.
