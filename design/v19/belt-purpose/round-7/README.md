band, "Love this, but would like to see it vertically. For the others, I
prefer the tile style." — "When I say this, I mean the repeat band".

- `l-tiles-vertical-band.html` — section and type are J's tiles,
  unchanged. The orbital period is K's band stood on end: one bordered
  strip, six equal cells from once at the top to 2 years at the bottom,
  hairlines between, the chosen cell filled in the accent, K's foot line
  beneath ("every year … then 29 Aug 2027", following the pointer or
  keys).

Scenes `?scene=`: `editing-period`, `editing-section`, `editing-type`,

rest. Packs by `?theme=`.

## Notes from the build

- Each cell reads figure and unit side by side ("1 year"): a wide, short
  cell is a row, and figure over unit left it mostly empty.
- Up/down (and left/right) walk the band, wrapping; Home/End; Enter or
  Space picks and closes; Escape closes; focus returns to the row.
- Orchestrator's fixes after review: the cells' text centred vertically
  (it sat high), and the chosen cell's unit at full strength (in clouds
  and dawn the faded unit fell below 4.5:1 on the dark accent).
- Click off still closes the key, the status drawer and the menu.
