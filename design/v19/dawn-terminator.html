orbit · v19 proposal · dawn / the terminator · the ratified temperature story with one line across it: night hands the sky to day, the stars on the far side come back as starlight, and the household constellations out there are drawn in it. The crossing is seated as a diagonal of the frame rather than in pixels off the dial, so the night keeps the same fifth-to-a-quarter of the sky on a phone, a laptop and a 32″ desk; the share and the tilt are rolled fresh every load, inside bounds that move the crossing but can never hide it, and the chart is held out of the dark by a guard on the dial itself.
