Feather — large and light

#1250, round 3. The name set the way a maker's name sits on a watch dial: large, light and quiet. Inter at its display optical size (the 'opsz 32' cut of the variable font the app already ships — tighter spacing, finer joins) at weight 300, sentence case, in --ink; desk 15 units (25 px), phone 19 units (18 px). Thin strokes need size, so the weight firms as the size falls (300 at 24 px and above, 400 by 13 px) and a long name drops to the largest size whose lines, broken at spaces and hyphens, fit the danger ring's chord. Hover firms the weight by 100: the name takes a breath when you reach for it.

Life: none at rest. Hover: the variable font's weight eases 300 → 400 over .35 s (Chromium animates font-weight on variable fonts). Reduced motion: the change is instant. Cost: the opsz cut of Inter, 71 KB once, against the 47 KB wght cut the app loads today — or the app could load the opsz cut instead of it, since it carries the weight axis too.
