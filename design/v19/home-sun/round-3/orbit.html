Orbit — the name on its own orbit

#1250, round 3. The name is written on an arc round the sun, concentric with the dial, the way a legend runs round the rim of an astrolabe or the bottom of a seal: the household is the orbit the sun holds. Space Grotesk 500 (the app's display face), sentence case, tracked .07em so the letters sit evenly on the curve, in --ink. The arc's radius comes from the name's measured length so the curve never exceeds about 115° (a long name takes two arcs, one inside the other, and drops a size to stay inside the danger ring). Hover warms the letters to the sun's own light and opens the tracking a touch.

Life: none at rest — the sun is the moving thing. Hover: fill and tracking ease over .3 s. Reduced motion: no transition. Costs one textPath per line and no extra font (Space Grotesk 500 is loaded by the app; at 13 KB it is already paid for).
