# #1250 — the household's name under the sun · round 3

Round 2 chose the **Furnace** sun at today's size (owner, 2026-10-06: "Actually,
furnace is the one"; "same scale"). The name was left as today — Inter 10 units
in `--ink-mid` on the desk (`+page.svelte:1519`), 15 units in the pocket
(`pocket.svelte:830`) — and the owner now says it is still too plain: "try and
improve the text in three different ways."

Round 1's three (Sunlit, Catalogue, Masthead — "None, they're all awful") are
not revisited: no name in the sun's colour at rest, no tracked mono capitals,
no crest with small labels. Each direction here is a different idea about the
type, not a colourway: where the name sits (Orbit), how big and how heavy it is
(Feather), which words carry the weight (Weighted). All three set the name in
`--ink` instead of today's `--ink-mid`, keep the sun and the name as one link,
and are generated by `build.py` from one scaffold (`python3 build.py`).

## What every page proves

The same crop as rounds 1 and 2: dial units 95–285 × 112–278 of the 380-unit
dial, desk 640 px dial (1.684 px/unit), phone 358 px dial (0.942 px/unit),
danger ring r=62, inner ring r=106, three static bodies. Packs: star chart,
dawn, retrograde (after dark and clouds follow by token). Names "The Lawsons"
and "The Fitzwilliam-Hargreaves Household". Hover (the desk's short name, each
pack) and reduced motion (the furnace still) are shot too.

The sun is round 2's Furnace carried forward verbatim: `furnace.html`'s shader
and glue, and its stills in `img/`, at the size the app ships — r=7 on the desk
and r=8 in the pocket (`web/src/lib/sun/furnace.js` `SUN_R`, `POCKET_SUN_R`).
The live canvas sits over the dial as `Sun.svelte` does, so the glow adds over
the top of the name exactly as it will in the app.

The name is measured and set in the browser, as the build would: each page
breaks the name at spaces and after hyphens, finds the largest size whose lines
fit the danger ring's chord at their baselines, and records what it chose in
`data-fit` on the link. On the phone the long name does not fit inside the ring
at any size above the floor in any direction (round 2 left the same third line
touching the ring), so a second pass sets it at the floor inside round 1's
~118-unit box and lets the last line reach the ring.

Fonts are the app's own, from `web/node_modules` into `fonts/`: Inter Variable
(wght), Inter's opsz cut (Feather only — the same package, a file the app does
not load today), Space Grotesk 500 and 600, JetBrains Mono (captions only).

## The three directions

### orbit.html — Orbit: the name on its own orbit
The name is written on an arc round the sun, concentric with the dial, as a
legend runs round the rim of an astrolabe or the bottom of a seal: the
household is the orbit the sun holds. Space Grotesk 500, sentence case, tracked
.07em (.06em on the phone) so letters sit evenly on the curve, `--ink`. The
arc's radius comes from the measured length so the curve stays near 110°
(desk short: 10.5 units on r=40); a long name takes two arcs and drops a size
(desk 9.5 units on r=39 and r=55; phone 13.5 on r=38 and r=54 at 150°, the
widest it goes). Hover warms the letters to the sun's light — `--sun` on the
dark packs, a text-grade amber on dawn — and opens the tracking by .02em.
Weakest where the name is long and the screen small: the phone's long name is
two tight arcs. Costs nothing new: Space Grotesk 500 is already loaded.

### feather.html — Feather: large and light
The name the way a maker's name sits on a watch dial: large, light, quiet.
Inter at its display optical size (`'opsz' 32`, the cut of the variable font the
app's package already carries), weight 300, sentence case, `--ink`; desk 15
units (25 px), phone 17 units (16 px — 19 did not clear the ring's chord). Thin
strokes need size, so the weight firms as the size falls (300 at 24 px and
above, 400 by 13 px): the desk's long name is 12 units at 334, the phone's 14
at 398. Hover firms the weight by 100 over .35 s (Chromium animates
`font-weight` on variable fonts); reduced motion makes it instant. Cost: the
opsz file is 71 KB against the 47 KB wght file the app loads — it carries the
weight axis too, so it could replace rather than add.

### weighted.html — Weighted: the family name carries the weight
One line, one face, one size, two weights: the family's name heavy (Inter 650,
`--ink`), the words round it — a leading "The", a trailing "Household" — light
(Inter 400, `--ink-mid`), as a book's spine gives the surname the weight and
lets "the" recede. Nothing is demoted to a label or a second line, which is
what separates it from Masthead. Desk 11 units (18.5 px), phone 15 (14 px); the
long name wraps to three lines on both, the weight running across the break.
Hover lifts the light words to `--ink`. The rule for which words are light:
exactly a leading "The" and a trailing "Household", "Family", "House" or
"Home"; a name with neither is all heavy. Costs nothing: the weight axis is
loaded.

## Recommendation

**Feather.** It answers "too plain" with presence rather than decoration, in a
voice the app already has (Inter, `--ink`), and it is the one whose short name
— the common case — looks best at both scales. Orbit is the most distinctive
and the most fragile: it is good on the desk's short name and strained on the
phone's long one. Weighted is the safest and the smallest change; its risk is
that the owner reads it as Masthead's idea again.
