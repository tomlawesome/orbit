Chromosphere — the Sun in the light of helium

#1250, round 2. The disc is SDO/AIA's 304 Å picture of the same day: the chromosphere's bright network, active regions, dark filaments, and prominences lifting off both limbs, kept beyond the disc as light; the inner corona's loops from AIA 171 Å beneath the streamers. Three frames an hour apart (12:08, 13:07, 14:09 UTC) are cross-faded, so what moves on the sun is the Sun's own motion. Graded to each pack: gold on the chart, amber ember on dawn, magenta on retrograde.

Life: a 36 s loop of the three frames, each fade 2.2 s. Reduced motion: the first frame only. Ships as three pictures per pack (17–20 KB each; dawn 15 KB); per frame, two compositor opacities.
