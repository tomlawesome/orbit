What’s new · Orbit 0.3.4

New in this release

  • Planets wear your section’s colour. Each section has a colour you choose; its items show it on the sky.
  • Size is cost or importance. Pick which one planet size shows, in Settings.
  • Items now have an importance. Your existing items say “not set” until you give them one.
  • Names up close. Planets due within 30 days show their name. Change the days in Settings.

For administrators

  • The first-run tour is off. New members land straight on the sky.
  • Notes like this one. Every release can carry a note for members and one for administrators; people see each once.
