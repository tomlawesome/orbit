**Everything in your orbit, on track.** A self-hosted home operations hub
for maintenance, renewals, recurring services, contracts, cover, and the
people who share them.

## Your home has an orbit.

Boilers need servicing. Insurance renews. Cars need inspections. Contracts
roll over. Orbit puts them all round one sun: your household. The nearer
something is, the sooner it's due. Done, and it swings back out to its next
date. Nothing slips past unseen.

## Done once. Remembered for good.

Complete it, and the next date is worked out for you. Every item keeps its
own rhythm: when it's due, how often it comes round, what it costs, when
you'll be reminded, and the documents that go with it. Reschedule or snooze
when life gets in the way.

## Forward, then file.

Every household has its own relay address. Forward a bill or a policy to
it. Orbit reads the attachment, works out what it is, and asks before
anything is added. Your mail stays in your mailbox; Orbit only ever reads a
copy.

## Shared, private, and yours to shape.

- **Share the load.** Add the people you live with by name. No invitations,
  no email addresses exposed.
- **Sections that fit.** Home, Vehicles, Devices and Services to begin with.
  Add, rename, reorder and recolour your own.
- **Make it yours.** Light or dark, five colourways, three text sizes.
- **A history for everything.** What was done and when, reminders before
  it's due, and its documents, stored encrypted.
- **On your home screen.** Installs like an app and can notify you. Your
  data stays on your server, not copied onto the phone.
- **Private by design.** Your own sign-in, or the identity provider you
  already use. Nothing is shared beyond your household.

## One app. Standard parts.

Three containers on a Linux machine of your own, set up by one command.
Nothing exotic to learn, nothing to babysit. Your data stays on your own
disk: records in the database, documents encrypted at rest. No paywall. No
plan. No account with us.
